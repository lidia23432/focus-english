# Semana 02 (A2) — Reading: Comparatives for options & priorities

## Objetivo (ES)
Leer comparativos en emails reales (decisiones, opciones, prioridades).

---

## Reading 1 — Scheduling options (internal)

**Subject:** Meeting options (timeline)

Hi team,  
Tuesday at 10:00 is **more convenient** for me than Monday at 9:00.  
If we need an **earlier** slot, I can do Tuesday at 9:00.

Thanks,  
Lidia

Questions:
1) Find two comparatives.  
2) Which option is recommended?

---

## Reading 2 — Priorities (external)

**Subject:** Next steps — priority and deadline

Hello Marta,  
This request is **more urgent** than the other items because it blocks our timeline.  
Option A is **faster** and **better** for delivery, but option B is **cheaper**.

Could you confirm your preference by Friday?

Kind regards,  
Lidia

Questions:
1) List the comparatives used.  
2) Why is “more urgent” used here?
