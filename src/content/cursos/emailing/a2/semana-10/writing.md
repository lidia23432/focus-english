# Semana 10 (A2) — Writing: Summary + action items (production)

## Objetivo (ES)
Escribir un email de cierre con resumen y action items (owners + deadlines).

---

## Task 1 — Convert to bullets
Convert this into 2–4 action items:

Text:
“We need to confirm the deadline, send the final version, and check the numbers.”

---

## Task 2 — Fill the template
Write 3 action items using:
- I will __ by __.
- Could you __ by __?
- Would you __ by __?

---

## Task 3 — Write a summary email (8–12 lines)
Situation: You had a short call about a project.

Include:
- Subject line (Summary + topic)
- 1–2 recap sentences (Past Simple)
- “Action items:” + 3 bullets (with owners + due dates)
- polite closing

---

## Checklist (A2)
- My summary is short ✅
- My action items have owners + deadlines ✅
- I use Could/Would politely ✅
