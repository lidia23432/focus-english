# Semana 10 (A2) — Grammar: Summary + action items (project emails)

## Objetivo (ES)
Cerrar un hilo con un resumen breve y **action items** claros (con responsable y deadline).

---

## 1) Short recap (A2)
Use Past Simple to recap:
- We discussed the timeline.
- I sent the updated draft.
- You confirmed the deadline.

Keep it short.

---

## 2) Action items structure (A2)
Simple and clear:
- **I will** + action + **by** + date
- **Could you** + action + **by** + date

Examples:
- I will send the final version by Friday.
- Could you confirm the VAT number by EOD?

✅ Tip: Use bullets for clarity.

---

## 3) Owners (who does what)
- **I** (you take responsibility)
- **We** (team responsibility)
- **You** (careful tone; use Could/Would)

---

## Mini practice (A2)

### A) Choose the best action item
1) (clear) I will send the file by Friday.  
2) (not clear) I send file Friday.

Best: 1)

### B) Rewrite (make it clear)
Rewrite:
1) You confirm tomorrow.  
2) I send the final.

Possible answers:
1) Could you confirm by tomorrow?  
2) I will send the final version by EOD.
