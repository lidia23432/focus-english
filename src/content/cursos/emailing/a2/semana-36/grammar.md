# Week 36 (A2) — Grammar: Final checklist (accuracy + tone)

## Objetivo (ES)
Checklist final para revisar tus emails A2 antes de enviar.

---

## A2 Accuracy checklist
✅ Requests:
- Could you send…? (no “to”)
- Would you confirm…?

✅ Verbs:
- Past Simple for updates: sent / got / did / made
- Correct third-person: confirms (only with he/she/it)

✅ Time:
- by Friday / by EOD
- on Tuesday / at 10:00

✅ Clarity:
- 1 purpose per email
- short sentences
- bullets for action items
