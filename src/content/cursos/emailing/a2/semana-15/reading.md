# Semana 15 (A2) — Reading: Reschedule (internal vs external)

## Objetivo (ES)
Leer 2 ejemplos de reprogramación con opciones.

---

## Reading 1 — Internal

**Subject:** Reschedule — call

Hi team,  
Sorry, I need to reschedule our call because I have a conflict.  
I’m available Wednesday at 11:00 or Thursday at 10:00.  
Which time works for you?

Thanks,  
Lidia

Questions:
1) What is the reason?  
2) What are the two options?

---

## Reading 2 — External

**Subject:** Apologies — reschedule our meeting

Hello Marta,  
I apologize, but I need to reschedule our meeting because I have another call.  
Would you be available on Wednesday at 11:00 or Thursday at 10:00?

Could you please confirm the best option?

Kind regards,  
Lidia

Questions:
1) Which polite request is used?  
2) How many options are offered?
