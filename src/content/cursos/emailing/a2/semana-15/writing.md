# Semana 15 (A2) — Writing: Reschedule email

## Objetivo (ES)
Escribir emails para reprogramar una reunión con razón y alternativas.

---

## Task 1 — Rewrite (more polite)
Rewrite:
1) Change the meeting.  
2) I can’t Tuesday.

---

## Task 2 — Internal reschedule (6–9 lines)
Include:
- apology
- reason (because)
- 2 options
- question: Which time works for you?

---

## Task 3 — External reschedule (7–10 lines)
Include:
- apology (I apologize…)
- 1 request with Would/Could
- 2 options
- request to confirm

---

## Checklist (A2)
- I apologize politely ✅
- I give a simple reason ✅
- I offer 2 options ✅
- I ask to confirm ✅
