# Semana 15 (A2) — Grammar: Rescheduling politely

## Objetivo (ES)
Reprogramar una reunión con tono profesional: disculpa + razón simple + nuevas opciones.

---

## 1) Polite rescheduling (A2)
- **Sorry, I need to reschedule our meeting.**
- **I’m not available** on Tuesday at 10:00.
- **Can we move it** to Wednesday?

External (more formal):
- **I apologize, but I need to reschedule our call.**
- **Would you be available** on Wednesday?

---

## 2) Give a simple reason (because)
- I need to reschedule **because** I have a conflict.
- I’m not available **because** I have another meeting.

---

## 3) Offer new options (2–3)
- How about Wednesday at 11:00 or Thursday at 10:00?
- Which time works better for you?

---

## Mini practice (A2)

### A) Choose the best option
1) Sorry, I need to ___ (reschedule / rescheduling)  
2) I need to reschedule because I ___ a conflict. (have / has)  
3) Would you be ___ on Thursday? (available / availability)

Answers:
1) reschedule  2) have  3) available

### B) Error correction
1) I need reschedule the call.  
2) Could you to confirm the new time?

Answers:
1) I need to reschedule the call.  
2) Could you confirm the new time?
