# Semana 15 (A2) — Vocabulary: Rescheduling & availability

## Objetivo (ES)
Vocabulario útil para cambiar reuniones sin sonar brusco.

---

## Key words
- **reschedule**
- **conflict**
- **available / not available**
- **slot**
- **option**
- **works for you**
- **apologize / sorry**

Useful phrases:
- Sorry, I need to reschedule.
- I have a conflict.
- Would you be available on…?
- Which time works better for you?

---

## Practice (A2)
Fill the gaps:
1) Sorry, I need to ___ our call.  
2) I have a ___.  
3) Would you be ___ on Thursday?

Answers:
1) reschedule  2) conflict  3) available
