# Week 24 (A2) — Vocabulary: Tone words & polite phrases

## Objetivo (ES)
Frases para modular el tono sin cambiar el contenido.

---

## Polite softeners

- **when possible**
- **if possible**
- **thanks in advance**
- **I appreciate your help** (A2 exposure)

Closings
- Thanks,
- Thank you,
- Kind regards,
- Best regards,
