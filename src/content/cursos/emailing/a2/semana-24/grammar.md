# Week 24 (A2) — Grammar: Tone control (internal vs external)

## Objetivo (ES)
Escribir el mismo mensaje con tono distinto: **interno (directo)** vs **externo (más formal)**.

---

## 1) Internal tone (clear & direct)
- Please send the updated file today.
- Can you confirm the deadline?

## 2) External tone (more polite)
- Could you please send the updated file today?
- Would you be able to confirm the deadline?

## 3) Softeners (A2)
- **just** (soft)
  - Just following up on…
- **when possible**
  - When possible, could you confirm…?
- **thanks in advance**

---

## Mini practice (A2)

### A) Choose internal vs external
Pick the best for an external email:
1) Please send the file.  
2) Could you please send the file?

Answer: 2)

### B) Rewrite (tone upgrade)
Rewrite to external tone:
1) Can you confirm by Friday?  
2) Please resend the link.

Possible answers:
1) Could you confirm by Friday, please?  
2) Could you please resend the link?
