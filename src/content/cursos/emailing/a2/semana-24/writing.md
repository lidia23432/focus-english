# Week 24 (A2) — Writing: Tone control

## Objetivo (ES)
Practicar reescritura por tono: interno vs externo.

---

## Task 1 — Upgrade the tone (external)
Rewrite these as external tone:
1) Please confirm receipt.  
2) Can you send the report by Friday?

---

## Task 2 — Downgrade the tone (internal)
Rewrite these as internal tone:
1) Would you be able to confirm the time?  
2) Could you please resend the link?

---

## Task 3 — Same message, two emails
Situation: You need an updated file and a deadline confirmation.

Write:
A) Internal email (4–6 lines)  
B) External email (5–7 lines)

---

## Checklist (A2)
- I choose the right request level ✅
- I use softeners appropriately ✅
- My closing matches the tone ✅
