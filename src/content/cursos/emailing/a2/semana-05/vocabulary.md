# Semana 05 (A2) — Vocabulary: Information requests

## Objetivo (ES)
Vocabulario para pedir datos que faltan (project-style).

---

## Key words
- **information**
- **details**
- **requirements**
- **access**
- **feedback**
- **questions**
- **clarification**

Useful phrases:
- Could you share some details?
- Do you have any questions?
- I need some information to continue.

---

## Practice (A2)
Fill the gaps:
1) I need some ___ to continue.  
2) Do you have any ___? (question/questions)  
3) Could you share some ___? (feedback/feedbacks)

Answers:
1) information/details  2) questions  3) feedback
