# Semana 05 (A2) — Reading: Asking for missing info

## Objetivo (ES)
Ver cómo se usan some/any en emails para pedir datos.

---

## Reading 1 — Internal (missing info)

**Subject:** Quick question — missing details

Hi team,  
Do we have **any** updates on the deadline?  
I need **some** information to finish the document.

Thanks,  
Lidia

Questions:
1) Find “any”. Why is it used?  
2) Find “some”. Why is it used?

---

## Reading 2 — External (polite request)

**Subject:** Request — additional information

Hello Carlos,  
Could you share **some** details about the requirements?  
If you have **any** questions, please let me know.

Kind regards,  
Lidia

Questions:
1) What does Lidia request?  
2) Is the tone internal or external?
