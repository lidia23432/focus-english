# Week 31 (A2) — Writing: Mini project thread (production)

## Objetivo (ES)
Escribir un hilo de proyecto completo (5 emails) con coherencia de tono y deadlines.

---

## Task — Write the full thread (A2)
Write 5 emails on the same topic (choose: report / invoice / meeting prep):

1) Request (external) — include deadline (by Friday)  
2) Update (internal) — Past Simple + next step (will)  
3) Delay (external) — apology + because/so + new ETA  
4) Follow-up (external) — polite chasing + timeframe (by EOD)  
5) Summary (internal) — recap + 3 action items (owners + due dates)

---

## Checklist (A2)
- Subjects are consistent ✅
- Deadlines/ETAs are clear ✅
- Tone is appropriate (internal/external) ✅
- Final summary includes action items ✅
