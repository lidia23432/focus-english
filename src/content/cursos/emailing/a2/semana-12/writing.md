# Semana 12 (A2) — Writing: Follow-up + alternatives

## Objetivo (ES)
Escribir follow-ups con “any update?” + timeframe + alternativa.

---

## Task 1 — Rewrite (soften the tone)
Make this more polite:
1) Send me the update.  
2) You didn’t reply.

---

## Task 2 — Write an internal follow-up (4–6 lines)
Include:
- Any update on…?
- 1 alternative (If needed, we can…)

---

## Task 3 — Write an external follow-up (6–9 lines)
Include:
- Just following up on…
- Could you share an ETA by Friday/by EOD?
- Another option is…

---

## Checklist (A2)
- My follow-up is polite ✅
- I include a timeframe ✅
- I propose an alternative ✅
