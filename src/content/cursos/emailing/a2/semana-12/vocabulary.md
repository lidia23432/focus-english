# Semana 12 (A2) — Vocabulary: Follow-up & options

## Objetivo (ES)
Vocabulario para “perseguir” con cortesía y proponer alternativas.

---

## Key words
- **follow-up**
- **update**
- **status**
- **ETA**
- **option**
- **alternative**
- **timeframe**
- **as soon as possible** (uso controlado)

Useful phrases:
- Any update on…?
- Could you share an ETA?
- Another option is…

---

## Practice (A2)
Fill the gaps:
1) Any ___ on the status?  
2) Could you share an ___?  
3) Another ___ is a call tomorrow.

Answers:
1) update  2) ETA  3) option
