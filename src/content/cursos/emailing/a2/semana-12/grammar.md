# Semana 12 (A2) — Grammar: Polite follow-up + alternatives

## Objetivo (ES)
Hacer follow-up con educación y ofrecer alternativas (opción A/B) para avanzar.

---

## 1) Polite follow-up phrases
- **Just following up on** __.
- **Any update on** __?
- **Could you share an ETA**?
- **When possible,** could you confirm __?

---

## 2) Proposing alternatives (A2)
Use:
- **If needed, we can…**
- **Another option is…**
- **Option A / Option B**

Examples:
- If needed, we can move the deadline to Monday.
- Another option is a short call tomorrow.

---
