# Week 20 (A2) — Writing: Give clarification (steps + example)

## Objetivo (ES)
Responder aclarando un punto y añadiendo pasos + ejemplo.

---

## Task 1 — Write one example line
Write:
- For example, …

(use: version / deadline / format)

---

## Task 2 — Internal clarification email (6–9 lines)
Situation: a teammate asked which file to use.
Include:
- To clarify…
- First/Then/Finally (or First/Then)
- For example, …
- closing (Let me know if you have questions)

---

## Task 3 — External clarification email (6–9 lines)
Situation: a client is confused about deadline and format.
Include:
- Just to clarify…
- deadline (by Friday/by EOD)
- 1 example
- polite closing

---

## Checklist (A2)
- I clarify clearly ✅
- I give steps ✅
- I include an example ✅
