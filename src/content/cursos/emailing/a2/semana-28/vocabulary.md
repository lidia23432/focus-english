# Week 28 (A2) — Vocabulary: Delivery & review

## Objetivo (ES)
Vocabulario para entregar, resumir cambios y pedir revisión.

---

## Key words
- **deliver**
- **draft**
- **final**
- **version**
- **changes**
- **review**
- **approve**
- **sign-off** (exposure)
- **receipt**

Useful phrases:
- Please find attached…
- Changes:
- Could you review…?
- Please confirm receipt.

---

## Practice (A2)
Fill the gaps:
1) Could you ___ the file by Friday?  
2) Please confirm ___.  
3) Changes: updated the ___.

Answers:
1) review  2) receipt  3) timeline
