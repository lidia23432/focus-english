# Week 28 (A2) — Reading: Delivery emails (internal vs external)

## Objetivo (ES)
Ver entregas con cambios y request de review.

---

## Reading 1 — Internal delivery

**Subject:** Delivery — report v2 (changes)

Hi team,  
Please find attached report v2.  
Changes:  
- Updated the timeline  
- Fixed page 2 numbers  

Could you review by Friday?

Thanks,  
Alex

Questions:
1) Which version is delivered?  
2) What is requested by Friday?

---

## Reading 2 — External final delivery

**Subject:** Final version attached — please confirm receipt

Hello Jordan,  
Here is the final version attached.  
Changes:  
- Added the VAT number  
- Updated the due date  

Could you confirm receipt by EOD, please?

Kind regards,  
Alex

Questions:
1) What is attached?  
2) What confirmation is requested?
