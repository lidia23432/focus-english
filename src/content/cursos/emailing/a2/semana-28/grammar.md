# Week 28 (A2) — Grammar: Delivery (versions + changes + review)

## Objetivo (ES)
Enviar una entrega (v1/v2/final), resumir cambios y pedir review con deadline.

---

## 1) Delivery lines
- Please find attached version v2.
- Here is the final version.

## 2) Change bullets (A2)
- Changes:
  - Updated the timeline.
  - Fixed the numbers.
  - Added the VAT number.

## 3) Review / sign-off request (A2)
- Could you review the attached file by Friday?
- Would you be able to confirm approval by EOD?

## 4) Confirm receipt
- Please confirm receipt.
- Could you confirm receipt by EOD?

---

## Mini practice (A2)
Choose the best:
1) Please find ___ the file. (attached/attach)  
2) Could you ___ by Friday? (review/reviews)  
3) Please confirm ___. (receipt/receive)

Answers:
1) attached  2) review  3) receipt
