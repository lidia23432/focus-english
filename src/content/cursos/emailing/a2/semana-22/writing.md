# Week 22 (A2) — Writing: Verify & correct details

## Objetivo (ES)
Escribir emails para confirmar datos y corregir un error de forma profesional.

---

## Task 1 — Write 4 confirmation questions
Write 4 lines (mix):
- Could you confirm…?
- Just to confirm, is…?

Use: date / number / address / deadline

---

## Task 2 — Correct a mistake (3–5 lines)
Situation: you sent the wrong date.
Write:
- apology
- correct detail
- request to confirm

---

## Task 3 — Verification email (7–10 lines)
Situation: you want to verify details before delivery.
Include:
- 3–4 bullets with details
- request to confirm
- deadline (by EOD/by Friday)

---

## Checklist (A2)
- Details are clear ✅
- Correction is polite ✅
- I request confirmation ✅
