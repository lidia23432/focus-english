# Week 22 (A2) — Vocabulary: Details & verification

## Objetivo (ES)
Vocabulario para verificar datos antes de cerrar una entrega.

---

## Key words
- **confirm**
- **correct**
- **wrong**
- **details**
- **number**
- **date**
- **address**
- **VAT number**
- **order number**

Useful phrases:
- Could you confirm…?
- The correct number is…
- Sorry, that was the wrong date.

---

## Practice (A2)
Fill the gaps:
1) The ___ date is 12/02.  
2) Could you ___ the VAT number?  
3) Sorry, the previous file was ___.

Answers:
1) correct  2) confirm  3) wrong
