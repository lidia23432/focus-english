# Week 22 (A2) — Reading: Confirm & correct details

## Objetivo (ES)
Leer emails de verificación (interno/externo) y corrección.

---

## Reading 1 — Internal (verify before sending)

**Subject:** Please confirm details (before delivery)

Hi team,  
Before we send the final report, could you confirm these details?  
- Deadline: Friday by EOD  
- Version: v2_final  
- Owner for approval: Sam  

Thanks,  
Alex

Questions:
1) How many details are listed?  
2) What is the deadline?
