# Week 22 (A2) — Grammar: Confirming & correcting details

## Objetivo (ES)
Confirmar datos (nombres, números, fechas) y corregir errores con cortesía antes de enviar algo.

---

## 1) Ask to confirm (A2)
- Could you confirm the VAT number?
- Please confirm the address.
- Just to confirm, is the deadline Friday?

## 2) Yes/No question forms
- Is the address correct?
- Is it 1482 or 1842?
- Is the meeting on Tuesday?

## 3) Correct politely (A2)
- The correct number is 1482.
- The correct date is 12/02.
- Sorry, the previous email had the wrong number.
