# Semana 14 (A2) — Vocabulary: Meeting confirmations

## Objetivo (ES)
Vocabulario esencial para confirmar reuniones y compartir materiales.

---

## Key words
- **confirm**
- **meeting**
- **agenda**
- **materials**
- **attachment**
- **link**
- **join** (online meeting)
- **attendance**

Useful phrases:
- This is to confirm our meeting…
- Agenda:
- Please find attached…
- Here is the link…

---

## Practice (A2)
Fill the gaps:
1) This is to ___ our meeting.  
2) Please find ___ the file.  
3) Here is the ___: __

Answers:
1) confirm  2) attached  3) link
