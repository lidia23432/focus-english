# Semana 14 (A2) — Grammar: Confirm a meeting + agenda + materials

## Objetivo (ES)
Confirmar una reunión con día/hora + agenda breve + materiales (adjunto/link).

---

## 1) Confirming a meeting (A2)
- This is to confirm our meeting on Tuesday at 10:00.
- Confirmed: meeting on Tuesday at 10:00.

## 2) Agenda (A2)
- Agenda:
  1) timeline
  2) next steps

## 3) Materials (attachments/links)
- Please find attached the updated agenda.
- Here is the link: __

## 4) Ask to confirm
- Could you please confirm?
- Please confirm attendance.

---

## Mini practice (A2)
Choose the best:
1) This is to ___ our meeting. (confirm / confirmation)  
2) Please find ___ the agenda. (attached / attach)  
3) Could you please ___ attendance? (confirm / confirmed)

Answers:
1) confirm  2) attached  3) confirm
