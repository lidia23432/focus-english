# Semana 14 (A2) — Reading: Meeting confirmation (internal vs external)

## Objetivo (ES)
Leer confirmaciones con agenda y materiales.

---

## Reading 1 — Internal

**Subject:** Confirmed — meeting Tuesday 10:00

Hi team,  
This is to confirm our meeting on Tuesday at 10:00.  
Agenda: 1) timeline  2) action items  
Please find attached the latest draft.

Thanks,  
Lidia

Questions:
1) What day and time is confirmed?  
2) What materials are included?

---

## Reading 2 — External

**Subject:** Meeting confirmation + agenda

Hello Marta,  
This is to confirm our call on Wednesday at 11:00.  
Agenda: 1) requirements  2) next steps  
Here is the meeting link: https://example.com

Could you please confirm attendance?

Kind regards,  
Lidia

Questions:
1) What is the agenda?  
2) What request is used at the end?
