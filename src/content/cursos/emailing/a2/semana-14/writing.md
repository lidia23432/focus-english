# Semana 14 (A2) — Writing: Confirm meeting + agenda + materials

## Objetivo (ES)
Escribir un email completo de confirmación de reunión (con agenda y materiales).

---

## Task 1 — Fix the mistakes
Correct:
1) This is to confirmation our meeting.  
2) Please find attach the agenda.

---

## Task 2 — Internal confirmation (6–9 lines)
Include:
- confirmation sentence (This is to confirm…)
- agenda (1–2 items)
- material (attached/link)
- short closing

---

## Task 3 — External confirmation (7–10 lines)
Include:
- confirmation sentence
- agenda
- link or attachment
- polite request to confirm attendance (Could/Would)
- professional closing

---

## Checklist (A2)
- I confirm day/time clearly ✅
- I add a short agenda ✅
- I include materials (attached/link) ✅
- I ask for confirmation politely ✅
