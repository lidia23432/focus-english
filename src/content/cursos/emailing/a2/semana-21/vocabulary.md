# Week 21 (A2) — Vocabulary: Attachments & versions

## Objetivo (ES)
Vocabulario para enviar adjuntos, versiones y cambios.

---

## Key words
- **attachment**
- **attached**
- **version**
- **draft**
- **final**
- **changes**
- **review**
- **confirm receipt**

Useful phrases:
- Please find attached…
- Attached: v2 / final
- Changes:
- Could you review…?

---

## Practice (A2)
Fill the gaps:
1) Please find ___ the updated file.  
2) This is version ___. (v1/v2/final)  
3) Could you ___ the attachment?

Answers:
1) attached  2) v2/final  3) review
