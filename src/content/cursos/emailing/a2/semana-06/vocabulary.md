## 3) Softener phrases (copy/paste)
- Just a quick follow-up on __.
- When you have a moment, could you __?
- Could you please __?
- Would you __, please?
- Thank you in advance.

## 4) Exercises (A2)

### A) Matching (phrase → meaning)
1) by Friday → a) before Friday (including Friday)
2) this week → b) in the current week
3) by end of day → c) before the workday ends

**Answers:** 1-a  2-b  3-c

### B) Fill in (follow-up / status / update / by)
1) Just a quick ______ on the invoice.  
2) Could you send an ______, please?  
3) Please reply ______ Friday.

**Answers:** 1) follow-up  2) update  3) by
