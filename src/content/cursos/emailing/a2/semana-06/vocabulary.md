# Semana 06 (A2) — Vocabulary: Follow-ups & timelines

## Objetivo (ES)
Vocabulario clave para follow-ups project-style.

---

## Key words
- **follow up**
- **update**
- **status**
- **ETA**
- **timeline**
- **deadline**
- **reminder** (gentle reminder)

Useful phrases:
- Just following up on…
- Could you share an ETA?
- Thanks for your help.

---

## Practice (A2)
Fill the gaps:
1) Could you share an ___?  
2) Any ___ on the timeline?  
3) Just following ___ on the invoice.

Answers:
1) ETA  2) update  3) up
