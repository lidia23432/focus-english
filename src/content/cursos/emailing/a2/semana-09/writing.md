# Semana 09 (A2) — Writing: Request a document + clear instructions

## Objetivo (ES)
Pedir un documento y añadir instrucciones claras con First/Then/Finally.

---

## Task A (Guided)
Write to Maria:
- request: Could you send the invoice for order 125, please?
- add 3 steps:
  - First…
  - Then…
  - Finally…
- ask: Please confirm receipt.

✅ 10–14 lines.

---

## Task B (Semi-guided)
Reply to Tom:
- confirm you attached the invoice
- ask to confirm receipt
- offer one extra document (report / updated file)

✅ 8–12 lines.

---

## Production task (Final)
Write ONE complete email (A2) that includes:
- 1 polite request using Could/Would
- 3 step instructions (First/Then/Finally)
- 1 time frame (by Friday / by end of day)
- 1 closing

---

## Extra exercises (for 2h/week)

### A) Politeness ladder (rewrite)
Rewrite “Please send the report.” in 3 versions:
- Can you…?
- Could you…?
- Would you…?

### B) Put steps in order
a) Finally, please confirm receipt.  
b) Then, please check the details.  
c) First, please open the document.

Answer: c → b → a

### C) Error correction
Correct:
1) Could you to send the document?  
2) First, please to check the file.  
3) Please confirms receipt.

Answers:
1) Could you send the document?
2) First, please check the file.
3) Please confirm receipt.

---
