# Semana 09 (A2) — Writing: Request + instructions

## Objetivo (ES)
Escribir un email pidiendo un documento y dando instrucciones claras.

---

## Task 1 — Rewrite
Rewrite these as polite requests (Could/Would):
1) Send the invoice.  
2) Confirm receipt.

---

## Task 2 — Order the steps
Put in order:
a) Finally, confirm by email.  
b) Then, check the details.  
c) First, open the document.

---

## Task 3 — Write an email (7–10 lines)
Situation: You need an updated file and want clear steps.

Include:
- 1 polite request (Could/Would)
- 3 steps (First/Then/Finally)
- request to confirm receipt
- professional closing

---

## Checklist (A2)
- I use First/Then/Finally ✅
- I use Could/Would correctly ✅
- I keep instructions short ✅
