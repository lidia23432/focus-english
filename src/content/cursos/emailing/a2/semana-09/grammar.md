# Semana 09 (A2) — Grammar: Clear instructions (First/Then/Finally) + polite requests

## Objetivo (ES)
Pedir un documento y dar instrucciones claras, paso a paso, con tono profesional.

---

## 1) Polite request forms (review)
- Please send the invoice.
- Can you send the invoice?
- Could you send the invoice, please?
- Would you send the invoice, please?

✅ A2 tip: **Could/Would** suenan más educados (ideal en emails externos).

---

## 2) Step-by-step instructions (A2)
Use connectors:
- **First,** …
- **Then,** …
- **Finally,** …

Examples:
- First, please open the file.
- Then, check page 2.
- Finally, confirm by email.

---

## 3) Confirm receipt (A2)
- Please confirm receipt.
- Could you confirm receipt, please?

---

## Mini practice (A2)

### A) Put in order
a) Finally, please confirm receipt.  
b) First, please open the document.  
c) Then, please check the details.

Answer: b → c → a

### B) Error correction
1) First, please to check the file.  
2) Could you to send the document?

Answers:
1) First, please check the file.  
2) Could you send the document?
