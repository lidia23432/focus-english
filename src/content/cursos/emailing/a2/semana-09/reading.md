# Semana 09 (A2) — Reading: Request documents + instructions

## Reading 1

**Subject:** Invoice request + next steps

Hello Maria,  
Could you send the **invoice** for order 125, please?  
First, please attach the invoice.  
Then, please confirm the total amount.  
Finally, please confirm receipt.  

Kind regards,  
Tom

### Comprehension (A2)
1) Tom requests…  
a) an invoice  b) a meeting  c) an address  

2) Tom uses…  
a) Could you  b) Could you to  c) Please confirms  

3) Tom uses step words…  
a) First/Then/Finally  b) Because/So  c) More/Than  

4) Tom asks Maria to…  
a) confirm receipt  b) pay now  c) cancel  

**Answers:** 1) a  2) a  3) a  4) a
