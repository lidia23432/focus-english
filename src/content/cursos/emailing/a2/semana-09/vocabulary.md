# Semana 09 (A2) — Vocabulary: Documents & instructions

## Objetivo (ES)
Vocabulario para pedir documentos y dar instrucciones simples.

---

## Documents
- **invoice**
- **report**
- **updated file**
- **attachment**
- **link**
- **version**

## Instruction verbs
- **open**
- **check**
- **confirm**
- **review**
- **send**
- **resend**

Practice:
1) Please ___ the attachment. (open/check)  
2) Could you ___ the report? (send/review)  
3) Please confirm ___. (receipt/version)

Suggested:
1) open  2) send/review  3) receipt
