# Week 23 (A2) — Reading: Progress update (internal vs external)

## Objetivo (ES)
Leer 2 updates cortos (interno/externo) con estado + ETA + next step.

---

## Reading 1 — Internal update

**Subject:** Quick update — timeline

Hi team,  
Quick update:  
- Status: we are **on track**.  
- ETA: Friday by EOD.  
- Next step: I will share the final file tomorrow.

Thanks,  
Alex

Questions:
1) What is the ETA?  
2) What is the next step?

---

## Reading 2 — External update (with blocker)

**Subject:** Update — invoice review

Hello Jordan,  
Status: we are **blocked** until we receive the updated invoice.  
Could you please send it by Friday? ETA for our review: Monday by EOD.

Thank you,  
Alex

Questions:
1) What is the blocker?  
2) What request is used?
