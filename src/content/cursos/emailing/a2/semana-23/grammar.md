# Week 23 (A2) — Grammar: Progress updates (status + ETA + next step)

## Objetivo (ES)
Escribir un **project update** corto con: estado, ETA y próximos pasos.

---

## 1) Status language (A2)
- **We are on track.**
- **We are blocked.**
- **We are waiting for** the invoice / the file / confirmation.

Examples:
- We are on track for Friday.
- We are blocked until we receive the updated file.

---

## 2) ETA + deadline (A2)
- **ETA:** Friday by EOD.
- by Friday / by EOD / tomorrow

Example:
- ETA: Friday by EOD.

---

## 3) Next steps (A2)
- **Next step:** I will send the final version by Friday.
- **Could you…?** (request help)
  - Could you confirm the deadline by EOD?

---

## Mini practice (A2)

### A) Choose the correct option
1) We are ___ (on track / on tracks).  
2) We are blocked until we ___ the file. (receive / receives)  
3) Could you ___ the ETA? (confirm / confirms)

Answers:
1) on track  2) receive  3) confirm

### B) Rewrite (make it project-style)
Rewrite:
1) The task is ok.  
2) I send tomorrow.

Possible answers:
1) We are on track.  
2) Next step: I will send it tomorrow.
