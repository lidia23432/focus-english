# Week 23 (A2) — Writing: Progress update email

## Objetivo (ES)
Escribir un update breve con estructura clara (status + ETA + next step).

---

## Task 1 — Build 4 bullets
Write 4 bullets using:
- Status:
- ETA:
- Next step:
- Request: Could you…?

---

## Task 2 — Internal update (6–9 lines)
Situation: you are on track.
Include:
- 2–3 bullets
- ETA
- next step with will

---

## Task 3 — External update (7–10 lines)
Situation: you are blocked waiting for a file.
Include:
- blocker sentence
- request with Could/Would + deadline (by Friday/by EOD)
- your ETA

---

## Checklist (A2)
- I use status language clearly ✅
- I include ETA + next step ✅
- My request is polite ✅
