# Semana 11 (A2) — Vocabulary: Decisions & recommendations

## Objetivo (ES)
Vocabulario para decidir y recomendar opciones en emails.

---

## Key words
- **recommend / suggestion**
- **option**
- **priority**
- **impact**
- **risk**
- **trade-off** (optional, A2 exposure)
- **deadline**
- **ETA**

Comparatives (review):
- **better / worse**
- **faster / slower**
- **cheaper / more expensive**
- **more convenient**
- **more urgent**

---

## Practice (A2)
Fill the gaps:
1) I ___ option A. (recommend/recommends)  
2) Option A is ___ than option B. (better/best)  
3) Could you confirm your ___ by Friday? (preference/preferences)

Answers:
1) recommend  2) better  3) preference
