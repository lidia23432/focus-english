# Semana 11 (A2) — Writing: Recommendation email

## Objetivo (ES)
Escribir un email recomendando una opción y pidiendo confirmación.

---

## Task 1 — Comparatives (quick)
Write the comparative:
1) good → ___  
2) convenient → ___  
3) cheap → ___  
4) urgent → ___

---

## Task 2 — Rewrite (add a reason)
Rewrite with “I recommend… because…”:
1) Option A is faster.  
2) Option B is cheaper.

---

## Task 3 — Write an email (8–12 lines)
Situation: You have two options:

- Option A: Friday (faster)
- Option B: Monday (safer)

Write an email including:
- 1 recommendation (I suggest/I recommend)
- 2 comparatives
- 1 reason with because
- 1 request (Could/Would you confirm…) + deadline (by Friday/by EOD)

---

## Checklist (A2)
- My recommendation is clear ✅
- I use comparatives correctly ✅
- I use because correctly ✅
- I include a deadline and a polite request ✅
