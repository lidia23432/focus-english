# Semana 11 (A2) — Grammar: Recommendations + comparatives (better option)

## Objetivo (ES)
Recomendar una opción en un email (project-style) usando comparativos y razones simples.

---

## 1) Recommendation phrases (A2-safe)
- **I suggest** option A.
- **I recommend** option A.
- **My suggestion is** option A.

✅ Tip: “I recommend” suena un poco más formal que “I suggest”.

---

## 2) Comparatives for decisions
Use comparatives to explain why:
- Option A is **better**.
- Option A is **faster**.
- Option A is **cheaper**.
- Option A is **more convenient**.
- Option A is **more urgent** (for priorities)

Examples:
- I recommend option A because it is **faster**.
- Option A is **better** than option B. (optional “than”)

---

## 3) Ask for confirmation (polite)
- **Could you confirm** your preference by Friday?
- **Would you confirm** which option you prefer?

---

## Mini practice (A2)

### A) Choose the best sentence
1) I recommend option A because it is faster. ✅  
2) I recommend option A because faster. ❌

### B) Make it a recommendation
Rewrite:
1) Option A is better.  
2) Option B is cheaper.

Possible answers:
1) I recommend option A because it is better.  
2) I suggest option B because it is cheaper.
