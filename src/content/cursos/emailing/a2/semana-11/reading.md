# Semana 11 (A2) — Reading: Recommendations (internal vs external)

## Objetivo (ES)
Ver cómo se recomienda una opción con comparativos y un motivo.

---

## Reading 1 — Internal (decision)

**Subject:** Recommendation — delivery option

Hi team,  
We have two options for delivery. I recommend option A because it is **faster** and **more convenient**.  
Option B is cheaper, but it is later.

Could you confirm your preference by Friday?

Thanks,  
Lidia

Questions:
1) Which option is recommended? Why?  
2) Find two comparatives.

---

## Reading 2 — External (polite recommendation)

**Subject:** Suggestion — next steps

Hello Carlos,  
I suggest option A because it is **better** for the timeline.  
Would you confirm which option you prefer by EOD?

Kind regards,  
Lidia

Questions:
1) Which polite request is used?  
2) What is the reason for option A?
