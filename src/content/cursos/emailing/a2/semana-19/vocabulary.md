# Week 19 (A2) — Vocabulary: Issues & support

## Objetivo (ES)
Vocabulario básico para describir problemas y pedir soporte.

---

## Key words
- **issue / problem**
- **error**
- **blocked**
- **access**
- **login**
- **attachment**
- **link**
- **resend**
- **support**

Useful phrases:
- We are blocked.
- I can’t access __.
- Could you help with __?
- Could you resend the link?

---

## Practice (A2)
Fill the gaps:
1) I can’t ___ the account.  
2) The link doesn’t ___.  
3) Could you ___ the attachment?

Answers:
1) access  2) work  3) resend
