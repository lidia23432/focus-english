# Week 19 (A2) — Reading: Help request (internal vs external)

## Objetivo (ES)
Ver estructura clara: issue → impact → request.

---

## Reading 1 — Internal help request

**Subject:** Help needed — access issue

Hi team,  
I can’t access the shared folder. The link doesn’t work on my side.  
We are blocked until we have access.

Could you help me check the permissions today?

Thanks,  
Alex

Questions:
1) What is the issue?  
2) What is the impact?  
3) What is the request?

---

## Reading 2 — External help request

**Subject:** Issue — missing attachment (please resend)

Hello Taylor,  
I can’t open the attachment from your last email, so I can’t proceed with the review.  
Could you resend the file, please?

Thank you,  
Alex

Questions:
1) Why can’t Alex proceed?  
2) Which request form is used?
