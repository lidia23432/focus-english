# Week 19 (A2) — Grammar: Asking for help (issue + impact + request)

## Objetivo (ES)
Pedir ayuda describiendo un problema con claridad: **qué pasa**, **impacto** y **petición**.

---

## 1) Describe the issue (A2)
Use simple sentences:
- We have an issue with the file.
- I can’t open the attachment.
- The link doesn’t work.
- I can’t access the account.

## 2) Add impact (project-style, A2)
- We are **blocked**.
- I **can’t proceed** without __.
- This affects the timeline.

Examples:
- I can’t open the attachment, so we are blocked.
- We are blocked until we receive the correct file.

## 3) Ask for help (polite)
- Could you help me with this?
- Could you resend the file, please?
- Would you be able to check the access?

✅ Tip A2: “Could you…?” suena profesional y evita sonar brusco.

---

## Mini practice (A2)

### A) Choose the correct option
1) The link doesn’t ___ (work/works).  
2) We are ___ (blocked/block).  
3) Could you ___ the file? (resend/resends)

Answers:
1) work  2) blocked  3) resend

### B) Rewrite (more polite)
Rewrite:
1) Fix this today.  
2) Send the file again.

Possible answers:
1) Could you help us with this today, please?  
2) Could you resend the file, please?
