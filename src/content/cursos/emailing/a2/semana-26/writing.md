# Week 26 (A2) — Writing: Correction email

## Objetivo (ES)
Escribir correcciones profesionales (archivo/fecha) y pedir confirmación.

---

## Task 1 — Fix the mistakes
Correct:
1) Sorry for send wrong file.  
2) The correct date are Friday.

---

## Task 2 — Internal correction email (6–9 lines)
Situation: you sent v1 but should send v2.
Include:
- apology
- correct version (v2)
- Just to confirm line
- request to confirm receipt

---

## Task 3 — External correction email (6–9 lines)
Situation: you sent the wrong meeting date.
Include:
- apology
- correct date
- Could/Would request to confirm
- polite closing

---

## Checklist (A2)
- I correct the mistake clearly ✅
- I use “Just to confirm” correctly ✅
- I ask for confirmation politely ✅
