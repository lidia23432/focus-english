# Week 26 (A2) — Grammar: Corrections (wrong file/date) + prevention

## Objetivo (ES)
Corregir errores en emails: disculpa breve + enviar lo correcto + confirmar detalles.

---

## 1) Apologize + correct (A2)
- Sorry — I sent the wrong file.
- Sorry, the previous email had the wrong date.
- Here is the correct file.

## 2) Prevent repeats (A2)
- Just to confirm, please use v2 (not v1).
- The correct date is Friday, 10 Jan.

## 3) Ask to confirm receipt
- Please confirm receipt.
- Could you confirm you received the correct file?
