# Week 26 (A2) — Vocabulary: Corrections & prevention

## Objetivo (ES)
Vocabulario para corregir errores y evitar confusiones.

---

## Key words
- **wrong**
- **correct**
- **previous**
- **updated**
- **version**
- **date**
- **receipt**

Useful phrases:
- Sorry, I sent the wrong file.
- Here is the correct version.
- Just to confirm, the correct date is…

---

## Practice (A2)
Fill the gaps:
1) Sorry, my ___ email had the wrong date.  
2) Here is the ___ file.  
3) Please confirm ___.

Answers:
1) previous  2) correct  3) receipt
