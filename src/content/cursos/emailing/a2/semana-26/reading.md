# Week 26 (A2) — Reading: Corrections (internal vs external)

## Objetivo (ES)
Ver correcciones claras con versión correcta y confirmación.

---

## Reading 1 — Internal correction

**Subject:** Correction — wrong file

Hi team,  
Sorry, I sent the wrong file in my previous email.  
Here is the correct version (v2).  
Just to confirm, please use v2 (not v1).

Please confirm receipt.

Thanks,  
Alex

Questions:
1) What was wrong?  
2) What version should they use?

---

## Reading 2 — External correction

**Subject:** Correction — meeting date

Hello Jordan,  
Sorry, my previous email had the wrong date.  
The correct date is Friday, 10 Jan. Could you please confirm?

Thank you,  
Alex

Questions:
1) What is the correct date?  
2) What request is used?
