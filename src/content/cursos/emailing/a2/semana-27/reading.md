# Week 27 (A2) — Reading: 3 follow-ups (thread)

## Objetivo (ES)
Ver un hilo de 3 emails (soft → normal → urgent).

---

## Reading 1 — Stage 1 (soft)

**Subject:** Follow-up — updated file

Hello Taylor,  
Just following up on the updated file. Any update on your side?  
Thanks in advance,  
Alex

Questions:
1) Which follow-up phrase is used?  
2) Is there a deadline?

---

## Reading 2 — Stage 2 (normal)

**Subject:** Follow-up — updated file (ETA)

Hello Taylor,  
Could you share an ETA for the updated file?  
If possible, could you confirm by Friday?

Thank you,  
Alex

Questions:
1) What is requested?  
2) What is the timeframe?

---

## Reading 3 — Stage 3 (urgent)

**Subject:** Urgent — timeline blocker (updated file)

Hello Taylor,  
This is a priority for us. We are blocked until we receive the updated file.  
To stay on schedule, could you send it by EOD today, please?

Thank you,  
Alex

Questions:
1) What is the impact?  
2) What is the deadline?
