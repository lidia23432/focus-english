# Week 27 (A2) — Grammar: 3-stage chasing (soft → normal → urgent)

## Objetivo (ES)
Aprender a hacer follow-up en 3 niveles, sin sonar agresivo, y con deadlines claros.

---

## 1) Stage 1 — Soft (very polite, low pressure)
- Just following up on __.
- Any update on __?
- When possible, could you confirm __?

## 2) Stage 2 — Normal (clear timeframe)
- Could you share an ETA?
- Could you confirm by Friday / by EOD?

## 3) Stage 3 — Urgent (impact + deadline, still polite)
- This is a priority for us.
- We are blocked until we receive __.
- To stay on schedule, could you send __ by EOD?

✅ Tip A2: “impact + deadline” suena firme sin ser borde.

---

## Mini practice (A2)

### A) Match the stage
1) “Just following up on the file.” → Stage ___  
2) “Could you confirm by Friday?” → Stage ___  
3) “We are blocked until we receive it.” → Stage ___

Answers:
1) 1  2) 2  3) 3

### B) Rewrite (make it softer)
Rewrite:
1) Reply today.  
Possible answer: Could you reply today, please?
