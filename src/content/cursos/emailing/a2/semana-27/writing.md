# Week 27 (A2) — Writing: 3-stage chasing pack

## Objetivo (ES)
Escribir 3 follow-ups del mismo tema, escalando el tono correctamente.

---

## Task 1 — Build 3 versions (same request)
Context: You are waiting for an updated invoice.

Write:
A) Stage 1 (soft) — 3–5 lines  
B) Stage 2 (normal) — 4–6 lines (include ETA + timeframe)  
C) Stage 3 (urgent) — 5–7 lines (include impact + deadline)

---

## Task 2 — Choose the best (tone)
Pick the best for an external email:
1) Send it now.  
2) Could you send it by EOD, please?

Best: 2)

## Checklist (A2)
