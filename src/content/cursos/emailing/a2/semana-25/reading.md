## Reading 1 — Internal (workaround)

**Subject:** Issue — proposed workaround

Hi team,  
We have an issue with the latest data, so the report is delayed.  
Another option is to deliver a draft today and the final version by Friday.

Could you confirm if this works?

Thanks,  
Alex

Questions:
1) What is the issue?  
2) What is the proposed solution?
