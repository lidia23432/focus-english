# Week 25 (A2) — Writing: Problem + solution email

## Objetivo (ES)
Escribir un email con problema, solución, plazo y confirmación.

---

## Task 1 — Build 4 lines
Write:
1) Problem: __  
2) Impact: __  
3) Solution: __  
4) Timeframe: __

---

## Task 2 — Write an internal email (7–10 lines)
Situation: issue delays the timeline.
Include:
- problem + impact
- 1 solution (I suggest / Another option is…)
- 1 timeframe
- request to confirm

---

## Task 3 — Write an external email (7–10 lines)
Situation: you must propose a new plan to a client/vendor.
Include:
- apology (optional)
- solution + deadline
- Would/Could request to confirm

---

## Checklist (A2)
- Problem is clear ✅
- Solution is clear ✅
- Deadline is clear ✅
- Tone is polite ✅
