# Week 25 (A2) — Vocabulary: Problems & solutions

## Objetivo (ES)
Vocabulario para describir problemas y proponer soluciones.

---

## Key words
- **issue**
- **cause** (A2 exposure)
- **impact**
- **solution**
- **workaround**
- **option**
- **deliver**
- **timeframe**

Useful phrases:
- We have an issue with…
- Another option is…
- We can deliver by…

---
