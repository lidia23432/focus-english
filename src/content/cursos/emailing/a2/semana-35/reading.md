# Week 35 (A2) — Reading: 8 scenario briefs (what you need to write)

## Objetivo (ES)
Leer 8 briefs y escribir un email para cada uno (interno/externo según indique).

---

## Scenario 1 — External request (deadline)
You need an updated invoice by Friday. Ask to confirm the VAT number.

## Scenario 2 — Internal update
You sent v2 yesterday. Give a quick status + next step by EOD.

## Scenario 3 — External delay
Apologize: the delivery is delayed because of an issue. Propose a new ETA.

## Scenario 4 — External follow-up (second message)
No reply yet. Ask for ETA and propose a timeframe.

## Scenario 5 — Internal summary + action items
After a meeting, write summary + 3 action items (owners + due dates).

## Scenario 6 — External meeting proposal
Propose a call with 2 options + mini agenda (2 items).

## Scenario 7 — External correction
You sent the wrong date/file. Correct it and ask to confirm.

## Scenario 8 — Polite urgency (blocked)
You are blocked waiting for a file. Ask politely for delivery by EOD.
