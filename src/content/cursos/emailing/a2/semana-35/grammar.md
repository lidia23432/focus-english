# Week 35 (A2) — Grammar: Final simulation rules (A2)

## Objetivo (ES)
Simulación final: aplicar A2 en situaciones reales con claridad y buen tono.

---

## Core rules (A2)
1) Use the right request level:
- Internal: Please / Can you
- External: Could you / Would you

2) Use deadlines clearly:
- by EOD / by Friday

3) Use structure:
- Short opening
- 2–4 key lines
- Clear closing

4) Use bullets when needed:
- Summary / Action items

Common mistakes to avoid:
- ❌ Could you to send…
- ✅ Could you send…
- ❌ Please confirms…
- ✅ Please confirm…
