# Semana 18 (A2) — Vocabulary: Reminders & checklists

## Objetivo (ES)
Vocabulario para recordatorios y checklist previos.

---

## Key words
- **reminder**
- **attendance**
- **receipt**
- **checklist**
- **agenda**

- Please confirm attendance/receipt.
- Checklist:
