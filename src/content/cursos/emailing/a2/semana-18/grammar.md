# Semana 18 (A2) — Grammar: Reminders + confirmations + checklist

## Objetivo (ES)
Enviar recordatorios cortos, pedir confirmación y añadir un checklist simple.

---

## 1) Reminder phrase (A2)
- **This is a reminder** about our meeting on Tuesday at 10:00.
- **Just a reminder:** deadline is Friday.

## 2) Asking to confirm (polite)
- Please confirm attendance.
- Could you please confirm receipt?
- Would you confirm if you can attend?

## 3) Checklist (simple bullets)
- Please bring the latest version.
- Please review the agenda.
- Please confirm the link works.

---

## Mini practice (A2)
Choose the best:
1) This is a ___ (reminder / remember)  
2) Please confirm ___ (attend / attendance)  
3) Would you ___ if you can attend? (confirm / confirmation)

Answers:
1) reminder  2) attendance  3) confirm
