# Semana 18 (A2) — Writing: Reminder email + checklist

## Objetivo (ES)
Escribir recordatorios (interno/externo) con checklist y confirmación.

---

## Task 1 — Write a checklist (3 items)
Write 3 checklist items using “Please + verb”.

---

## Task 2 — Internal reminder (5–8 lines)
Include:
- This is a reminder…
- checklist (2–3 items)
- closing

---

## Task 3 — External reminder (6–9 lines)
Include:
- Just a reminder…
- deadline (by Friday/by EOD)
- request to confirm (Could/Would)
- polite closing

---

## Checklist (A2)
- My reminder is short ✅
- My checklist is clear ✅
- I request confirmation politely ✅
