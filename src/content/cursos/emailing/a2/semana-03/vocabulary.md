## 4) Exercises (A2)

### A) Matching (verb → past)
1) send → a) received  
2) receive → b) sent  
3) check → c) checked  
4) reply → d) replied  

**Answers:** 1-b  2-a  3-c  4-d

### B) Fill in (sent/received/checked/replied)
1) I ______ your email.  
2) I ______ the file today.  
3) I ______ the document.  
4) I ______ yesterday. (optional)

**Possible answers:** 1) received  2) sent  3) checked  4) replied

### C) Build 6 update sentences (A2)
Write 6 short update sentences using:
sent / received / checked / confirmed / today / tomorrow
