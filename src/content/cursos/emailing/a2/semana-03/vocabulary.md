# Semana 03 (A2) — Vocabulary: Updates & status verbs

## Objetivo (ES)
Tener verbos y frases típicas para dar updates de proyecto en emails.

---

## 1) Verbs for updates
- **sent** (send)
- **received / got** (receive/get)
- **checked** (check)
- **updated** (update)
- **fixed** (fix)
- **completed** (complete)
- **reviewed** (review)

Examples:
- I **sent** the file this morning.
- We **fixed** the issue.
- I **reviewed** the draft.

---

## 2) Project-style phrases (A2)
- **Quick update:** …
- **Status:** …
- **Next step:** …
- **ETA:** …
- **We are on track.** (simple)
- **We are blocked.** (simple)

---

## Practice (A2)
Fill the gaps:
1) Quick ___: I sent the file.  
2) Next ___: I will share the final version.  
3) I ___ your email yesterday. (got/gets)

Answers:
1) update  2) step  3) got
