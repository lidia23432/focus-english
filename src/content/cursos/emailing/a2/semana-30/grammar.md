# Week 30 (A2) — Grammar: Meeting pack (propose / confirm / remind / reschedule)

## Objetivo (ES)
Tener el “pack” completo de reuniones: proponer, confirmar, recordar y reprogramar.

---

## 1) Propose (2 options)
- Are you available Tuesday at 10:00 or Wednesday at 11:00?
- Which time works better for you?

## 2) Confirm + agenda + link
- This is to confirm our call on Tuesday at 10:00.
- Agenda: 1) timeline  2) next steps
- Here is the link: __

## 3) Reminder + checklist
- This is a reminder about our meeting tomorrow.
- Checklist: please review the agenda.

## 4) Reschedule (polite)
- Sorry, I need to reschedule because I have a conflict.
- Would you be available on…?
