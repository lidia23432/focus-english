# Week 30 (A2) — Reading: Meeting thread (4 emails)

## Objetivo (ES)
Leer un hilo completo: propose → confirm → reminder → reschedule.

---

## Reading 1 — Propose (external)

**Subject:** Meeting options — next steps

Hello Jordan,  
Would you be available for a short call next week?  
I’m available Tuesday at 10:00 or Wednesday at 11:00.  
Which time works better for you?

Kind regards,  
Alex

---

## Reading 2 — Confirm

**Subject:** Meeting confirmed — Tuesday 10:00

Hello Jordan,  
This is to confirm our call on Tuesday at 10:00.  
Agenda: 1) timeline  2) action items  
Here is the link: https://example.com

Please confirm attendance.

Thanks,  
Alex

---

## Reading 3 — Reminder

**Subject:** Reminder — meeting tomorrow

Hello Jordan,  
Just a reminder: our call is tomorrow at 10:00.  
Checklist: please review the agenda and test your microphone.

Thank you,  
Alex

---

## Reading 4 — Reschedule

**Subject:** Apologies — reschedule our call

Hello Jordan,  
Sorry, I need to reschedule because I have a conflict.  
Would you be available Wednesday at 11:00 or Thursday at 10:00?

Kind regards,  
Alex

1) List the 4 email types.
