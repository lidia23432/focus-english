# Week 30 (A2) — Writing: Meeting pack (4 emails)

## Objetivo (ES)
Escribir tu pack de reuniones completo (4 emails cortos).

---

## Task 1 — Write 4 subjects
Write 4 subject lines:
1) Propose  
2) Confirm  
3) Reminder  
4) Reschedule

---

## Task 2 — Write the 4 emails (A2)
Write 4 short emails (each 5–8 lines):
1) Propose (2 options)  
2) Confirm (agenda + link)  
3) Reminder (checklist)  
4) Reschedule (apology + 2 new options)

---

## Checklist (A2)
- I propose options clearly ✅
- I confirm with agenda/link ✅
- I remind politely ✅
- I reschedule with apology + options ✅
