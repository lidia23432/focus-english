# Week 29 (A2) — Grammar: Requests pack (Please / Can / Could / Would)

## Objetivo (ES)
Dominar la “escalera” de cortesía y elegir la forma adecuada según el contexto.

---

## 1) Ladder (review)
- Please send the file. (direct)
- Can you send the file? (neutral)
- Could you send the file? (more polite)
- Would you send the file? (very polite)

## 2) Common errors
✅ Could you send…?  
❌ Could you to send…?

✅ Please confirm receipt.  
❌ Please confirms receipt.

---

## Mini practice (A2)
