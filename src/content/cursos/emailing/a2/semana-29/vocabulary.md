# Week 29 (A2) — Vocabulary: Request verbs (core set)

## Objetivo (ES)
Verbos clave para pedir acciones en emails (pack).

---

## Verbs
- send / share
- confirm
- check / review
- resend
- update
- approve (exposure)

Practice:
1) Could you ___ the deadline?  
2) Please ___ the file.  
3) Can you ___ the link?

Suggested:
1) confirm  2) send/share  3) resend
