# Week 29 (A2) — Reading: Mixed requests (internal vs external)

## Objetivo (ES)
Identificar qué request se usa y por qué.

---

## Reading 1 — Internal (direct)

**Subject:** Quick request

Hi team,  
Please check the latest file.  
Can you confirm the deadline by EOD?

Thanks,  
Alex

Questions:
1) Which request forms are used?  
2) Why is this OK internally?

---

## Reading 2 — External (more polite)

**Subject:** Request — updated invoice

Hello Taylor,  
Could you please resend the updated invoice?  
Would you confirm the VAT number by Friday?

Thank you,  
Alex

Questions:
1) Find two polite requests.  
2) What deadline is mentioned?
