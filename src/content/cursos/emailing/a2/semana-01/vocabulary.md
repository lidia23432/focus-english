# Semana 01 (A2) — Vocabulary: Requests & polite email verbs

## Objetivo (ES)
Tener un vocabulario base para pedir acciones de forma profesional y clara.

---

## 1) Request verbs (common)
- **send** (enviar)
- **share** (compartir)
- **check** (revisar / comprobar)
- **confirm** (confirmar)
- **review** (revisar en detalle)
- **resend** (reenviar)
- **update** (actualizar)
- **approve** (aprobar)
- **provide** (proporcionar)

Examples:
- Could you **share** the latest version?
- Please **confirm** the deadline.
- Can you **resend** the link?

---

## 2) Polite words & softeners (A2-safe)
- **please**
- **just** (para suavizar): “Just a quick question…”
- **quick**: “a quick update”
- **when possible** (cuando sea posible)
- **thanks / thank you**
- **thanks in advance** (gracias por adelantado)

Examples:
- Just a quick question: could you confirm the time?
- Please send it when possible.

---

## 3) Mini glossary (project-style)
- **deadline** = fecha límite
- **ETA** = estimated time of arrival (fecha/hora estimada)
- **next steps** = próximos pasos
- **action items** = tareas/acciones

---

## Practice (A2)
Fill the gaps:
1) Could you ___ the updated file? (send/share)  
2) Please ___ receipt. (confirm/check)  
3) Can you ___ the link? I can’t open it. (resend/update)

Answers:
1) send/share  2) confirm  3) resend
