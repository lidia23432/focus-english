# Semana 01 (A2) — Grammar: Requests ladder (Please / Can / Could / Would)

## Objetivo (ES)
Aprender a **diferenciar el nivel de cortesía** en peticiones de email de trabajo y elegir la opción adecuada (interno vs externo).

---

## 1) The politeness ladder (A2)

From more direct → more polite:

1) **Please + verb** (direct but still polite)
- Please send the updated file.
- Please confirm the meeting time.

2) **Can you + verb…?** (neutral)
- Can you send the updated file?
- Can you confirm the meeting time?

3) **Could you + verb…?** (more polite / more professional)
- Could you send the updated file?
- Could you confirm the meeting time?

4) **Would you + verb…?** (very polite)
- Would you send the updated file?
- Would you confirm the meeting time?

### Quick rule (ES)
- **Interno**: “Please / Can you” suele ser suficiente (claro y rápido).
- **Externo** (cliente/proveedor): “Could you / Would you” suena más profesional.

---

## 2) Common mistakes (A2)

✅ Correct:
- Could you **send** the file? (NOT “to send”)
- Would you **confirm** receipt?
- Please **confirm** receipt. (NOT “confirms”)

❌ Incorrect:
- Could you **to send** the file?
- Would you **confirms** receipt?

---

## 3) Mini practice (A2)

### A) Choose the best option (internal vs external)
1) (Internal, quick) ___ you send the link? (Can / Could / Would)  
2) (External, polite) ___ you confirm receipt, please? (Can / Could / Would)

Suggested answers:
1) Can / Could  
2) Could / Would

### B) Rewrite (make it more polite)
Rewrite using **Could you**:
1) Can you check the numbers?  
2) Please send the invoice.

Possible answers:
1) Could you check the numbers?  
2) Could you send the invoice?

### C) Error correction
Correct these:
1) Could you to resend the file?  
2) Please confirms receipt.

Answers:
1) Could you resend the file?  
2) Please confirm receipt.
