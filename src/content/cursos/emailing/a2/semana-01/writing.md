# Semana 01 (A2) — Writing: Requests ladder (production)

## Objetivo (ES)
Escribir emails cortos con **requests correctos** y elegir el **tono** (interno/externo).

---

## Task 1 — Rewrite (ladder practice)
Rewrite each request in **4 levels**:

Original:
1) Send the file.  
2) Confirm the meeting time.  
3) Check the numbers.  

Write:
A) Please + verb  
B) Can you…?  
C) Could you…?  
D) Would you…?

---

## Task 2 — Write 2 short emails (internal + external)

### A) Internal email (4–6 lines)
Situation: You need an updated report today. Ask for:
- the updated report
- confirmation of the deadline

Include:
- 1 request with **Can you…?**
- 1 request with **Please…**

### B) External email (5–7 lines)
Situation: You need a vendor to resend a link and confirm receipt.
Include:
- 1 request with **Could you…?**
- 1 request with **Would you…?** (or a second Could you)

---

## Checklist (A2)
- I don’t use “to” after Could/Would ✅
- I choose the tone (internal vs external) ✅
- I keep the email short and clear ✅
- I include a clear closing ✅
