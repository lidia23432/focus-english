# Semana 01 (A2) — Reading: Requests (internal vs external)

## Objetivo (ES)
Leer 2 emails cortos y notar **tono** + **estructura** + **requests ladder**.

---

## Reading 1 — Internal (neutral, quick)

**Subject:** Quick request: updated deck

Hi Ana,  
Can you send the updated deck today?  
Please include the final slide with the timeline.

Thanks,  
Lidia

### Questions
1) Which request is used? (Please / Can / Could / Would)  
2) Is the tone internal or external?

---

## Reading 2 — External (more polite)

**Subject:** Request — Updated invoice (Order #1482)

Hello Carlos,  
Could you please send the updated invoice for Order #1482?  
If possible, could you include the VAT number on the document?

Thank you in advance,  
Lidia Martínez

### Questions
1) Find two polite requests.  
2) Why is this tone appropriate for an external email?

---

## Quick notice (ES)
Internal emails can be shorter and more direct. External emails usually sound more formal and polite.
