# Semana 07 (A2) — Reading: Meeting options with comparatives

## Reading 1

**Subject:** Meeting options — what is better?

Hello Maria,  
Can we meet **on Thursday at 9:00** or **on Thursday at 10:00**?  
Is **9:00** **better** for you than **10:00**?  
Could you confirm, please?  

Kind regards,  
Tom

### Comprehension (A2)
1) Tom offers…  
a) one time  b) two times  c) three times  

2) Tom asks if 9:00 is…  
a) better than 10:00  b) later than 10:00  c) more expensive  

3) Tom uses…  
a) Could you confirm  b) Could you to confirm  c) Please confirms  

4) The meeting day is…  
a) Thursday  b) Tuesday  c) Monday  

**Answers:** 1) b  2) a  3) a  4) a

## Reading 2

**Subject:** Re: Meeting options

Hi Tom,  
Yes, **9:00** is **better** for me.  
10:00 is too late.  
Please confirm.  

Best regards,  
Maria

### Comprehension (A2)
1) Maria chooses…  
a) 9:00  b) 10:00  c) both  

2) Maria says 10:00 is…  
a) too late  b) too early  c) perfect  

3) Maria asks Tom to…  
a) confirm  b) pay  c) resend  

4) Tone is…  
a) polite  b) angry  c) rude  

**Answers:** 1) a  2) a  3) a  4) a
