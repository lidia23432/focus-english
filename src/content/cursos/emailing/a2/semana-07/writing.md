# Semana 07 (A2) — Writing: Clarifying & confirming

## Objetivo (ES)
Escribir emails con preguntas claras para evitar errores de proyecto.

---

## Task 1 — Make it polite (rewrite)
Rewrite as polite questions:
1) Explain the deadline.  
2) Tell me the owner.  
3) I don’t understand “final”.

---

## Task 2 — Write an internal email (5–7 lines)
Situation: you need to confirm 3 details:
- deadline
- file version (v1/v2)
- meeting time

Include:
- “Just to confirm…”
- 2–3 questions

---

## Task 3 — Write an external email (6–9 lines)
Situation: you need clarification from a vendor/client.
Include:
- 1 request with “Could you clarify…?”
- 1 confirmation question (Just to confirm…)
- a polite closing

---

## Checklist (A2)
- My questions are short ✅
- I use “Just to confirm” correctly ✅
- I use Could you…? politely ✅
