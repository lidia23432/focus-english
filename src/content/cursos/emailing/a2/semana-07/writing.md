# Semana 07 (A2) — Writing: Meeting options with comparatives

## Objetivo (ES)
Proponer 2 opciones de hora y usar comparativos para negociar de forma simple.

---

## Task A (Guided)
Write to Maria:
- Offer 2 options on the same day:
  - Thursday at 9:00
  - Thursday at 10:00
- Ask: Is 9:00 better than 10:00?
- Request confirmation using **Could you…?**

✅ 8–12 lines.

---

## Task B (Semi-guided)
Reply to Tom:
- Choose one option (9:00)
- Say the other is too late / not convenient
- Ask to confirm (Please confirm / Would you confirm…)

✅ 7–11 lines.

---

## Production task (Final)
Write ONE complete email (A2) that includes:
- 2 meeting options
- 1 comparative sentence (better / more convenient / earlier / later)
- 1 polite request (Could you / Would you)
- closing

---

## Extra exercises (for 2h/week)

### A) Rewrite using comparatives
Rewrite:
1) Can we meet early? → (earlier)  
2) Wednesday is good. → (better)  
3) Friday is convenient. → (more convenient)

Possible answers:
1) Can we meet earlier?
2) Wednesday is better.
3) Friday is more convenient.

### B) Choose the best form (politeness)
Pick the best option for a neutral email:
- Please confirm.
- Can you confirm?
- Could you confirm?
- Would you confirm?

Suggested answer: Could you confirm?

### C) Error correction
Correct:
1) Tuesday is gooder than Wednesday.  
2) Can we meet more earlier?  
3) Could you to confirm the time?

Answers:
1) Tuesday is better than Wednesday.
2) Can we meet earlier?
3) Could you confirm the time?
