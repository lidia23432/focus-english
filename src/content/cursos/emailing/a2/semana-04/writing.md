# Semana 04 (A2) — Writing: Next steps email (will vs going to)

## Objetivo (ES)
Escribir un email de “next steps” con compromisos claros y deadlines.

---

## Task 1 — Choose will / going to
1) I ___ send the file by Friday.  
2) We ___ review the draft tomorrow.  
3) I ___ update the plan next week.

---

## Task 2 — Write 6 sentences (A2)
Write:
- 3 sentences with **will**
- 3 sentences with **going to**
Use project vocabulary (timeline, draft, meeting, next steps).

---

## Task 3 — Write an email (7–10 lines)
Situation: Project plan for next week.

Include:
- “Next steps:” + 3 bullets
- at least 1 **will** commitment with a deadline
- at least 1 **going to** plan (tomorrow/next week)
- 1 polite request (Could you…? / Would you…?) to confirm something

---

## Checklist (A2)
- I use will/going to correctly ✅
- I include deadlines (by Friday/by EOD) ✅
- My steps are clear and short ✅
