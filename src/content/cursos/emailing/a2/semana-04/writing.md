# Semana 04 (A2) — Writing: Next steps (will / going to) + polite requests

## Objetivo (ES)
Escribir emails con “next steps” claros:
- 2–3 acciones con **will/going to**
- 1 request educado (Could/Would)
- timeline (today/tomorrow/next week)

---

## Task A (Guided) — Next steps email
Write to Maria:
- I will check the document today.
- I will send an update tomorrow.
- request: Could you confirm receipt, please?

✅ 7–12 líneas. Usa subject + closing.

---

## Task B (Semi-guided) — Reply + plan
Reply to Tom:
- Thank you, I received it.
- We are going to meet next week.
- request: Would you send the agenda, please?

✅ 7–12 líneas.

---

## Production task (Final)
Write ONE complete email (A2) that includes:
- 2 “will” actions (next steps)
- 1 “going to” plan
- 1 polite request (Could/Would)
- 1 closing + name

---

## Extra exercises (for 2h/week)

### A) Choose will / going to
1) I ____ send the file today.  
2) We are ____ meet on Friday.  
3) I ____ reply tomorrow.

Suggested answers: will / going to / will

### B) Error correction
Correct:
1) I will to send the update tomorrow.  
2) We are going meet next week.  
3) Would you confirms receipt?

Answers:
1) I will send the update tomorrow.  
2) We are going to meet next week.  
3) Would you confirm receipt?

### C) Politeness ladder (rewrite one request)
Rewrite in 4 ways:
“Send the agenda.”
- Please…
- Can you…?
- Could you…?
- Would you…?
