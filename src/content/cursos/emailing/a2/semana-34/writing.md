# Week 34 (A2) — Writing: Build your A2 template kit

## Objetivo (ES)
Crear tu kit final A2 para copiar/pegar.

---

## Task 1 — Write 12 subject lines
Write:
- 3 requests (with deadlines)
- 3 updates (with ETA)
- 3 summaries (with next steps)
- 3 reminders (with deadlines)

---

## Task 2 — Create your opening/closing list
Write:
- 5 openings (internal/external mixed)
- 6 closings (neutral/formal)

---

## Task 3 — Action items templates
Write 6 action items using:
- Owner + verb + by + date
- Could you + verb + by + date

---

## Checklist (A2)
- Subjects are clear ✅
- Templates are reusable ✅
- Action items include owners + due dates ✅
