Crear plantillas A2 reutilizables con estructura consistente.

## 1) Subject patterns
- Subject: Request — __ by __
- Subject: Update — __ (ETA __)
- Subject: Summary + next steps — __
- Subject: Reminder — __ (deadline __)

## 2) Openings

- Hi team,
External:
- Hello __,

## 3) Closings

- Thank you,
- Kind regards,
- Best regards,
