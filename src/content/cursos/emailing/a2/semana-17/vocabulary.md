# Semana 17 (A2) — Vocabulary: Online meetings & tech issues

## Objetivo (ES)
Vocabulario básico para reuniones online y problemas técnicos.

---

## Key words
- **link**
- **join**
- **click**
- **microphone**
- **camera**
- **access**
- **doesn’t work**
- **resend**

Useful phrases:
- Here is the meeting link: __
- I can’t join the meeting.
- Could you resend the link, please?

---

## Practice (A2)
Fill the gaps:
1) Please ___ the link.  
2) I can’t ___ the meeting.  
3) Could you ___ the link?

Answers:
1) click  2) join  3) resend
