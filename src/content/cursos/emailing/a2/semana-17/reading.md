# Semana 17 (A2) — Reading: Link + issue

## Objetivo (ES)
Leer un email enviando link y otro reportando un problema y pidiendo reenvío.

---

## Reading 1 — Sending the link (internal)

**Subject:** Meeting link + instructions

Hi team,  
Here is the meeting link: https://example.com  
First, please click the link. Then, join the meeting.  
Please check your microphone before we start.

Thanks,  
Lidia

Questions:
1) List two instructions.  
2) What should you check?

---

## Reading 2 — Problem + resend (external)

**Subject:** Issue — meeting link

Hello Carlos,  
I can’t join the meeting. The link doesn’t work on my side.  
Could you resend the link, please?

Thank you,  
Lidia

Questions:
1) What is the issue?  
2) What request is used?
