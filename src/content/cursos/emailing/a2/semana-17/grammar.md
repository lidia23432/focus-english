# Semana 17 (A2) — Grammar: Online meeting links + troubleshooting

## Objetivo (ES)
Enviar un link de reunión con instrucciones claras y reportar problemas simples.

---

## 1) Sharing a link (A2)
- Here is the meeting link: __
- Please join using this link: __

## 2) Simple instructions (imperatives)
- Please **click** the link.
- Please **join** the meeting.
- Please **check** your microphone.

## 3) Reporting issues (A2)
- I can’t join the meeting.
- The link doesn’t work.
- I can’t access the call.

## 4) Polite request to resend
- Could you resend the link, please?
- Would you share a new link, please?

---

## Mini practice (A2)
Choose the correct:
1) The link doesn’t ___ (work/works)  
2) Could you ___ the link? (resend/resends)  
3) Please ___ the meeting. (join/joined)

Answers:
1) work  2) resend  3) join
