# Semana 16 (A2) — Grammar: Urgency (polite escalation)

## Objetivo (ES)
Pedir algo urgente de forma educada, explicando impacto y deadline.

---

## 1) Polite urgency (A2)
Useful phrases:
- **This is a priority for us.**
- **We are blocked until** we receive __.
- **To stay on schedule,** we need __ by Friday.

✅ Tip: explicas el motivo (impact) y suena menos agresivo.

---

## 2) Polite but firm requests
- Could you please send the file by EOD?
- Would you be able to confirm the deadline by Friday?

Avoid rude tone:
- ❌ “Send it now.”
- ✅ “Could you send it by EOD, please?”

---

## 3) Timeframes
- by Friday
- by EOD
- today / tomorrow

---

## Mini practice (A2)

### A) Choose the best (polite)
1) ___ you send it by EOD, please? (Can/Could/Would)  
Suggested: Could / Would

### B) Rewrite (less aggressive)
Rewrite:
1) Send the update today.  
2) This is urgent. Reply now.

Possible answers:
1) Could you send the update today, please?  
2) This is urgent. Could you reply by EOD, please?
