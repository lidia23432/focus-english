# Semana 16 (A2) — Reading: Polite urgency (internal vs external)

## Objetivo (ES)
Ver urgencia con tono correcto y deadlines claros.

---

## Reading 1 — Internal (blocker)

**Subject:** Blocker — need confirmation by EOD

Hi team,  
We are blocked until we confirm the deadline.  
Could you please confirm by EOD? This is a priority for us.

Thanks,  
Lidia

Questions:
1) What is the blocker?  
2) What is the deadline?

---

## Reading 2 — External (polite escalation)

**Subject:** Follow-up — urgent request (timeline)

Hello Carlos,  
Just following up. To stay on schedule, we need the updated invoice by Friday.  
Would you be able to send it by Friday, please?

Thank you,  
Lidia

Questions:
1) What is needed by Friday?  
2) Which request form is used?
