# Semana 16 (A2) — Vocabulary: Urgency & blockers

## Objetivo (ES)
Vocabulario para urgencias y bloqueos en proyectos.

---

## Key words
- **urgent**
- **priority**
- **blocked**
- **timeline**
- **on schedule**
- **as soon as possible** (uso controlado)
- **by EOD / by Friday**

Useful phrases:
- We are blocked until we receive __.
- This is a priority for us.
- To stay on schedule, we need __ by __.

---

## Practice (A2)
Fill the gaps:
1) We are ___ until we receive the file.  
2) This is a ___.  
3) Please send it by ___.

Answers:
1) blocked  2) priority  3) EOD/Friday
