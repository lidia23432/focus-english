# Semana 16 (A2) — Writing: Urgency without rudeness

## Objetivo (ES)
Escribir un email urgente (interno o externo) manteniendo un tono profesional.

---

## Task 1 — Fix the tone
Rewrite politely:
1) Reply now.  
2) Send it today.

---

## Task 2 — Internal urgency (5–8 lines)
Include:
- blocker phrase (We are blocked until…)
- deadline (by EOD)
- polite request (Could/Would)

---

## Task 3 — External urgency (7–10 lines)
Include:
- short context (timeline)
- deadline (by Friday)
- Would/Could request
- polite closing

---

## Checklist (A2)
- I sound polite ✅
- I explain impact (blocked/timeline) ✅
- I include a clear deadline ✅
