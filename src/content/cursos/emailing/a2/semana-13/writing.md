# Semana 13 (A2) — Writing: Scheduling with options

## Objetivo (ES)
Escribir emails proponiendo reunión con 2–3 opciones y agenda corta.

---

## Task 1 — Rewrite (more natural)
Rewrite:
1) I no available Monday.  
2) I can’t Monday morning.

---

## Task 2 — Internal email (5–7 lines)
Include:
- 1 constraint (I can’t…)
- 2 options
- “Which time works for you?”

---

## Task 3 — External email (6–9 lines)
Include:
- 1 polite request (Could/Would)
- 2–3 options
- mini agenda (1–2 items)

---

## Checklist (A2)
- I state constraints clearly ✅
- I give 2–3 options ✅
- I include a question to choose ✅
