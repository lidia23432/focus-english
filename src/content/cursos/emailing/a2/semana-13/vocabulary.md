# Semana 13 (A2) — Vocabulary: Scheduling words

## Objetivo (ES)
Vocabulario útil para proponer reuniones y elegir horarios.

---

## Key words
- **available / not available**
- **free / busy**
- **slot**
- **option**
- **works for you**
- **schedule**
- **agenda**

Useful phrases:
- I’m available on __ at __.
- I can’t do __.
- Which time works better for you?

---

## Practice (A2)
Fill the gaps:
1) I’m ___ on Tuesday at 10:00.  
2) I’m not ___ on Monday morning.  
3) Which time ___ for you?

Answers:
1) available  2) available  3) works
