# Semana 13 (A2) — Grammar: Availability + constraints (scheduling)

## Objetivo (ES)
Decir disponibilidad y limitaciones (I can / I can’t) y proponer opciones de reunión.

---

## 1) Availability (A2)
- I’m available on Tuesday at 10:00.
- I’m free tomorrow afternoon.

## 2) Constraints (A2)
- I can’t do Monday morning.
- I’m not available at 9:00.

## 3) Offering options
- How about Tuesday at 10:00 or Wednesday at 11:00?
- Which time works better for you?

---

## Mini practice (A2)
Choose the best:
1) I ___ available on Tuesday. (am / do)  
2) I can’t ___ Monday morning. (do / doing)  
3) Which time ___ for you? (works / work)

Answers:
1) am  2) do  3) works
