# Semana 35 — Grammar (A1): Full review checklist

## Objetivo (ES)
Revisión global A1 (no gramática nueva). Enfócate en errores típicos.

---

## A1 must-check
- Subject present
- Hello/Hi + name
- on + day, at + time
- Please + verb (no -s)
- Can you + verb (no -s)
- short sentences (1 idea per sentence)
- closing + comma (Kind regards,)

## Mini test (10)
Corrige (escribe la versión correcta):
1) Please confirms.  
2) Can you sends it, please?  
3) We meet at Monday.  
4) We meet on 10:00.  

Answers:
1) Please confirm.
2) Can you send it, please?
3) We meet on Monday.
4) We meet at 10:00.
