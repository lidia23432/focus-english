# Semana 35 — Reading (A1): 5 mini-cases (Mon–Fri)

Read the cases and choose the best purpose.

## Case 1 (Mon)
You need information about a service. You ask for price + details.

Purpose:
a) request info  b) confirm meeting  c) resend file  
Answer: a

## Case 2 (Tue)
You propose a meeting on Wednesday at 10:00.

Purpose:
a) attachment  b) meeting proposal  c) thanks  
Answer: b

## Case 3 (Wed)
You send a document as an attachment and ask to confirm receipt.

Purpose:
a) attachment  b) follow-up  c) reminder  
Answer: a

## Case 4 (Thu)
You ask: “Any update on the invoice?”

Purpose:
a) follow-up  b) introduction  c) problem  
Answer: a

## Case 5 (Fri)

a) reminder  b) problem + resend  c) meeting confirmation
