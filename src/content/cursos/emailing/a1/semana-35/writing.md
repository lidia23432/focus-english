# Semana 35 — Writing (A1): Workweek simulation (Mon–Fri)

## Objetivo (ES)
Escribir 5 emails A1 (uno por día). Usa tu kit de plantillas.

---

## Monday — Request info
Write an email:
- I need information about __.
- Please send price + details.
- Please confirm.

---

## Tuesday — Meeting proposal
Write an email:
- Can we meet on __ at __?
- Is this time OK for you?
- Please confirm.

---

## Wednesday — Attachment
Write an email:
- Please find attached __.
- Please confirm receipt.

---

## Thursday — Follow-up
Write an email:
- Any update on __?
- Please let me know.
- Thank you.

---

## Friday — Problem + resend
Write an email:
- I can’t open the file.
- Can you resend it, please?
- Please confirm.

---

## Production task (Final)
Write the 5 emails with:
- subject + greeting + closing
- 1 idea per sentence
- correct on/at

---

## Extra: Self-check after each email
- Subject ✅
- on/at ✅
- Please/Can you + verb ✅
- short sentences ✅
- closing ✅
