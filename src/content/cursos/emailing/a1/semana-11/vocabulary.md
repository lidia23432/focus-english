# Semana 11 — Vocabulary (A1): Missing info

## 1) Core words (16)
need, have, document, file, invoice, address, phone number, details, information, confirm, send, help, today, tomorrow, correct, missing

## 2) Useful chunks
- Do you have __?
- Do you need __?
- Yes, I do. / No, I don’t.
- Please send __.
- Please confirm.

## 3) Exercises
A) Matching (have/need/missing/confirm → ES)
B) Gap-fill (12): have/need/do/don’t
C) Build 6 short Q&A pairs.
