# Emailing A1 — Semana 11 — Writing
**Tema:** Follow-up simple + because  
**Duración:** 25–35 min

## Plantilla (A1)
**Subject:** Follow-up

Hi [NAME],  
This is a follow-up.  
Do you have the [ITEM]?  
Please send it, because I need it today.  
Thank you.  
Best regards,  
[YOUR NAME]

---

## Tarea 1 (principal)
- Destinatario: **Taylor**
- Tu nombre: **Sam**
- Item: **price list**

---

## Tarea 2 (variación)
Escribe otro follow-up para:
- Item: **schedule**
- Cambia la razón:
  - “because I need it this week.”

(Frase A1: this week)

---

## Checklist ✅
- Subject correcto
- Opening: “This is a follow-up.”
- 1 pregunta “Do you have…?”
- 1 request + because (razón simple)
- Thank you + cierre
- 6–9 líneas
