# Semana 04 — Reading (A1): What / Where / Who in emails

## Reading 1

**Subject:** Questions about the meeting

Hello Maria,  
What is the meeting room?  
Where is the office?  
Who is the contact person?  
Please let me know.  

Kind regards,  
Ana

### Comprehension (A1)
1) Ana asks…  
a) 1 question  b) 3 questions  c) 0 questions  

2) Ana asks about…  
a) salary  b) meeting details  c) holidays  

3) Ana says…  
a) Please let me know  b) Please pay  c) Please cancel  

4) The email is…  
a) short  b) very long  c) not clear  

**Answers:** 1) b  2) b  3) a  4) a

---

## Reading 2

**Subject:** Re: Questions about the meeting

Hi Ana,  
The meeting room is **Room 3**.  
The office is **on the 2nd floor**.  
The contact person is **Tom**.  
Please confirm.  

Best regards,  
Maria

### Comprehension (A1)
1) The meeting room is…  
a) Room 2  b) Room 3  c) Room 30  

2) The office is…  
a) on the 1st floor  b) on the 2nd floor  c) on the 3rd floor  

3) The contact person is…  
a) Tom  b) Ana  c) Maria  

4) Maria asks Ana to…  
a) confirm  b) cancel  c) call now  

**Answers:** 1) b  2) b  3) a  4) a
