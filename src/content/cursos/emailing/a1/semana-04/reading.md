# Emailing A1 — Semana 04 — Reading (Consolidación 1)
**Tema:** Email completo A1 (presentación + request + horarios)  
**Duración:** 10–15 min

## Texto (A1)
**Subject:** Information and meeting time

Hi Jordan,  
My name is Alex. I work at Working English Lab.  
Can you send the price list, please?  
Can we meet on Monday at 10:00?  
Or on Tuesday at 14:00?  
Thank you.  
Best regards,  
Alex

## Preguntas
1) Who is the email to?  
A) Alex  B) Jordan  C) Working English Lab

2) Alex asks for:  
A) the schedule  B) the price list  C) a phone number

3) Alex proposes:  
A) 1 time  B) 2 times  C) 3 times

4) Which day is an option?  
A) Friday  B) Monday  C) Sunday

5) Which time is an option?  
A) 08:00  B) 12:00  C) 14:00

6) “Best regards” is:  
A) opening  B) closing  C) question

## Soluciones
1) B  
2) B  
3) B  
4) B  
5) C  
6) B
