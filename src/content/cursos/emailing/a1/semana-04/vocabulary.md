# Semana 04 — Vocabulary (A1): Basic information at work

## Objetivo (ES)
Vocabulario para pedir información básica por email (A1): lugar, contacto, datos y sala.

---

- room

- details

- Who is the contact person?

- Please let me know.
- Thank you for your help.

---

## 3) Exercises (A1)

### A) Matching (word → category)
Write the category: **place / person / information**
1) address → ___  
2) manager → ___  
3) meeting room → ___  
4) phone number → ___  

**Answers:** 1) information  2) person  3) place  4) information

### B) Fill in the gaps
1) Where is the ________? (office / manager)  
2) Who is the ________ person? (contact / floor)  
3) What is the ________ room? (meeting / email)

**Answers:** 1) office  2) contact  3) meeting

### C) Mini vocabulary review (10 min)
Write 6 short sentences using:
- address / office / meeting room / contact person
Example: “The office is on the second floor.”
