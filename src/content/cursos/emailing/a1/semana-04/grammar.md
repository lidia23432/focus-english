# Emailing A1 — Semana 04 — Grammar (Consolidación 1)
**Tema:** Repaso: to be + Can you…? + on/at  
**Duración:** 10–15 min

## Recordatorio rápido
- **My name is… / I am… / I work at…**
- **Can you send… please?**
- **on** + day (on Monday)  
- **at** + time (at 10:00)

## Ejercicios

### A) Completa con am / is / are
1) My name ___ Alex.  
2) I ___ from Spain.  
3) I ___ a coordinator.

### B) Completa con on / at
4) on Monday ___ 10:00  
5) on Tuesday ___ 14:00

### C) Escribe la frase (usa las palabras)
6) can / you / send / the price list / please  
7) can / we / meet / on Monday / at 10:00

### D) Elige la mejor opción (A o B)
8) A) Send the price list.  B) Can you send the price list, please?  
9) A) Meet Monday at 10:00?  B) Can we meet on Monday at 10:00?

## Soluciones
1) is  
2) am  
3) am  
4) at  
5) at  
6) Can you send the price list, please?  
7) Can we meet on Monday at 10:00?  
8) B  
9) B
