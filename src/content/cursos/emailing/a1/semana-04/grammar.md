# Semana 04 — Grammar (A1): What / Where / Who questions (work emails)

## Objetivo (ES)
Aprender a pedir información básica en emails de trabajo con preguntas cortas:
- **What is…?** (qué es / cuál es)
- **Where is…?** (dónde está)
- **Who is…?** (quién es)

---

## 1) Question patterns (A1)

### What is...?
- What is your phone number?
- What is the meeting room?

### Where is...?
- Where is the office?
- Where is the meeting room?

### Who is...?
- Who is the contact person?
- Who is my manager?

✅ Tip A1: 1 pregunta por línea.

---

## 2) Short answers (A1)

- The office is in Madrid.
- The meeting room is Room 2.
- The contact person is Tom.

---

## 3) Polite phrases (A1)

- Please help me.
- Please confirm.
- Thank you for your help.
- Please let me know.

---

## 4) Mini practice (A1)

### A) Make questions (reorder)
1) is / What / your / email / ?  
2) is / Where / the / office / ?  
3) is / Who / the / contact / person / ?

**Answers**
1) What is your email?  
2) Where is the office?  
3) Who is the contact person?

### B) Choose the best question word
1) ___ is the address? (What / Where)  
2) ___ is the office? (What / Where)  
3) ___ is the manager? (Who / Where)

**Answers:** 1) What  2) Where  3) Who

### C) Error correction
1) What your email is?  
2) Where the office is?  
3) Who is contact person?

**Possible answers**
1) What is your email?  
2) Where is the office?  
3) Who is the contact person?
