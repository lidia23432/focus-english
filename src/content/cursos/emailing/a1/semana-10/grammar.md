# Semana 10 — Grammar (A1): Present Simple (I/We) — office hours

## Objetivo (ES)
Escribir emails informativos con horarios usando Present Simple (I/We).

---

## 1) Present Simple (A1)
- We open at 9:00.
- We close at 5:00.
- We work Monday to Friday.

## 2) Simple questions
- What are your office hours?
- What time do you open?
- What time do you close?

## 3) Mini practice
A) Gap-fill (10)
1) We ___ at 9:00. (open)
2) We ___ at 5:00. (close)

B) Error correction (6)
1) We opens at 9:00.
2) We close at 5:00? (statement form)

Answers:
1) We open at 9:00.
2) We close at 5:00.
