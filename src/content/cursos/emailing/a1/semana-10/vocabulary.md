# Semana 10 — Vocabulary (A1): Office hours

## 1) Core words (16)
office hours, open, close, start, finish, work, Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, morning, afternoon, available, schedule, contact

## 2) Useful chunks
- Our office hours are __.
- We open at __.
- We close at __.
- We work Monday to Friday.
- Please let me know.

## 3) Exercises
A) Matching: open/close/start/finish → ES
B) Gap-fill: open/close + times (12 items)
C) Build 6 short sentences describing hours.
