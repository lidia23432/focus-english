# Semana 02 — Vocabulary (A1): Contact & availability

## Objetivo (ES)
Vocabulario y “chunks” listos para emails de trabajo: contacto, disponibilidad y confirmación.

---

## 1) Core words (15)
- phone number
- extension
- email address
- contact
- available
- not available
- office
- hours
- call
- meeting
- today
- tomorrow
- morning
- afternoon
- confirm

---

## 2) Useful chunks (copy/paste)
- My email is __.
- My phone number is __.
- My extension is __.
- I am available on __ at __.
- I am not available on __.
- Are you available on __ at __?
- Please confirm.
- Thank you.

---

## 3) Exercises (A1)

### A) Matching (word → meaning in Spanish)
1) available → ___  
2) extension → ___  
3) phone number → ___  
4) confirm → ___  

**Answers (example):**
1) disponible  2) extensión  3) número de teléfono  4) confirmar

### B) Fill in
1) My ______ is 204.  
2) I am ______ on Monday at 10:00.  
3) Please ______.

**Possible answers:** 1) extension  2) available  3) confirm

### C) Make 6 short sentences
Use these chunks:
- My email is __.
- My phone number is __.
- I am available on __ at __.
Write 6 short sentences (A1).
