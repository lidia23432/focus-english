# Semana 15 — Vocabulary (A1): Rescheduling

## 1) Core words (16)
sorry, change, reschedule, new time, available, not available, meeting, call, time, day, confirm, OK, today, tomorrow, problem, update

## 2) Useful chunks (copy/paste)
- Sorry for the change.
- I am not available on __ at __.
- Can we meet on __ at __?
- How about __ at __?
- Is this time OK for you?
- Please confirm.

## 3) Exercises
A) Matching (sorry/change/reschedule/available → ES)
B) Gap-fill (12): sorry / not available / can we / how about / confirm
C) Build 6 short reschedule sentences.
