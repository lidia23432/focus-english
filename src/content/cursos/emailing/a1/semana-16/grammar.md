# Semana 16 — Grammar (A1): Two options with OR

## Objetivo (ES)
Ofrecer **2 opciones** (día/hora) y pedir que elijan.

## 1) OR (A1)
- Tuesday at 10:00 **or** Wednesday at 11:00.

## 2) Useful questions
- Can we meet on Tuesday at 10:00 **or** on Wednesday at 11:00?
- Which time is OK for you?
- Please confirm.

## Mini practice
A) Fill in: or / on / at
1) Tuesday __ 10:00 __ Wednesday __ 11:00
Answer: at / or / at

B) Reorder
you / is / Which / OK / time / for / ?
→ Which time is OK for you?
