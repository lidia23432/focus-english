# Semana 09 — Vocabulary (A1): Receipt & thanks

## 1) Core words (16)
received, got, thanks, thank you, email, file, document, check, review, today, tomorrow, confirm, OK, great, help, update

## 2) Useful chunks
- Thanks, I received the file.
- I got your email.
- Thank you for the document.
- I will check it today.
- I will review it tomorrow.

## 3) Exercises
A) Matching (received/got/check/review → ES)
B) Gap-fill (12 items): received / got / today / tomorrow
C) Build 6 short reply sentences.
