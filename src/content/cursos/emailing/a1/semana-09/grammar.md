# Semana 09 — Grammar (A1): Confirm receipt + next step

## Objetivo (ES)
Responder a un email para:
- confirmar recepción (I received / I got)
- agradecer (Thank you / Thanks)
- decir siguiente paso (I will check today/tomorrow)

---

## 1) Fixed phrases (A1)
- Thanks, I received the file.
- I got your email.
- Thank you for the document.

## 2) Next step (A1)
- I will check it today.
- I will review it tomorrow.

## 3) Mini practice

A) Choose: received / got
1) Thanks, I ___ the file. (received/got)
2) I ___ your email. (received/got)

Answers: 1) received  2) got (or received)

B) Choose: today / tomorrow
1) I will check it ___.  
2) I will review it ___.

Answers: (any order) today/tomorrow

C) Error correction
1) I received your email yesterday. (A1 version)
2) I will checks it today.

Possible answers:
1) I received your email. I will check it today.
2) I will check it today.
