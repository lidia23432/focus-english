# Semana 07 — Vocabulary (A1): Requests

## 1) Core words (16)
information, details, price, quote, product, service, order, delivery, date, time, address, document, send, confirm, help, update

## 2) Useful chunks (copy/paste)
- I need information about __.
- Please send me the details.
- Please send the price.
- Can you help me, please?
- Please confirm.
- Thank you for your help.

## 3) Exercises (A1)

A) Matching (EN → ES)
1) quote  2) delivery  3) details  4) confirm
(Respuestas posibles: presupuesto, entrega, detalles, confirmar)

B) Fill in
1) I need ______ about the service.
2) Please send the ______.
3) Please ______.

C) Build 6 short sentences
Use: I need / please send / details / price / confirm / thank you
