# Semana 07 — Grammar (A1): Requests (Please… / I need…)

## Objetivo (ES)
Pedir información simple en un email de trabajo usando:
- **Please + verb**
- **I need...**
- pedir confirmación

---

## 1) Please + verb (A1)
- Please send the price.
- Please send the details.
- Please confirm.

Tip: 1 acción por línea.

---

## 2) I need... (A1)
- I need information.
- I need the address.
- I need the invoice.

---

## 3) Mini practice

### A) Choose the correct word
1) Please ___ the details. (send / sends)  
2) I ___ information. (need / needs)  
3) Please ___. (confirm / confirmation)

Answers: 1) send  2) need  3) confirm

### B) Error correction
1) I needs the price.  
2) Please sending the details.  
3) Please confirms.

Answers:
1) I need the price.  
2) Please send the details.  
3) Please confirm.
