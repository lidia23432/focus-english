# Semana 13 — Grammar (A1): Availability + How about…?

## 1) Available / not available
- I am available on Monday at 10:00.
- I am not available on Monday.
- I am not available at 10:00.

## 2) Propose an alternative
- How about Tuesday at 11:00?
- Can we meet on Tuesday at 11:00?

## 3) Mini practice
A) Choose on/at
1) available __ Monday (on/at) → on
2) available __ 10:00 (on/at) → at

B) Error correction
I am not available at Monday.
→ I am not available on Monday.
