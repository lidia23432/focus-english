# Semana 34 — Writing (A1): Your template kit

## Objetivo (ES)
Crear tu banco personal de plantillas A1 para escribir rápido.

---

## Task A — 10 subject lines
Write 10 subject lines for:
1) introduction
2) request info
3) contact details
4) attachment
5) invoice request
6) meeting options
7) meeting confirmation
8) reminder
9) follow-up
10) problem

---

## Task B — 10 request sentences
Write 10 short requests (A1). Use:
- Please + verb
- Can you…?, please?

Examples:
- Please confirm.
- Can you resend it, please?

---

## Production task (Final) — Your A1 kit
Create your final kit (copy/paste ready):
- 10 subjects
- 6 openings
- 10 requests
- 6 closings
- your signature block (name + role)

---

## Extra exercises
A) Rewrite: make 6 sentences shorter (A1 style)  
B) Error correction: 10 lines (Please confirms / Can you sends…)  
C) Checklist: short, clear subject, polite closing
