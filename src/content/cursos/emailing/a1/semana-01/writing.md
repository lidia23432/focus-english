# Emailing A1 — Semana 01 — Writing
**Tema:** Email de presentación (6–8 líneas)  
**Duración:** 15–25 min

## Plantilla (A1)
**Subject:** Hello from [NAME]  
Hi [NAME],  
My name is [YOUR NAME].  
I am from [CITY/COUNTRY].  
I work at [COMPANY]. I am a [ROLE].  
Nice to meet you.  
Best regards,  
[YOUR NAME]

## Tu tarea
Escribe un email a una persona nueva:

- Destinatario: **Chris**
- Tu nombre: **Lidia**
- Ciudad/País: **Barcelona, Spain**
- Empresa: **Working English Lab**
- Rol: **Training Coordinator**

## Checklist ✅
- Subject claro
- “Hi + nombre”
- “My name is…”
- “I am from…”
- “I work at… / I am a…”
- Cierre: “Best regards,” + tu nombre
- Frases cortas (A1) y sin emojis

## Ejemplo (modelo)
**Subject:** Hello from Lidia  
Hi Chris,  
My name is Lidia.  
I am from Barcelona, Spain.  
I work at Working English Lab. I am a Training Coordinator.  
Nice to meet you.  
Best regards,  
Lidia
