# Semana 01 — Writing (A1): Your first work introduction email

## Objetivo (ES)
Escribir un email corto (A1) para presentarte con estructura profesional.

---

## 1) Template (copy/paste)

**Subject:** Introduction

Hello __,  
My name is __.  
I am a __.  
I work at __.  
I am in the __ department.  
Nice to meet you.  

Kind regards,  
__

---

## 2) Task A (Guided)
Complete the template with:
- Name: Ana (o tu nombre)
- Job: assistant
- Company: Focus English
- Department: Support
- Recipient: Tom

✅ 6–8 líneas máximo.

---

## 3) Task B (Semi-guided)
Write a reply to Ana:
- Welcome
- Your name
- You are her contact person
- Offer help

✅ 5–7 líneas.

---

## 4) Production task (Final)
Write your own introduction email (A1) to a new colleague.
Rules:
- 1 idea per sentence
- 5–7 sentences
- Add subject + closing

---

## 5) Extra exercises (for 2h/week)

### A) Reorder (put in correct order)
1) Kind regards,  
2) My name is Sara.  
3) Subject: Introduction  
4) Hello Mr. Brown,  
5) I am a new team member.

**Answer order:** 3 → 4 → 2 → 5 → 1

### B) Make it polite (rewrite)
Rewrite with “please” or “thank you”:
1) Send me your phone number.  
2) Answer my email.

Possible answers:
1) Please send me your phone number. Thank you.  
2) Please answer my email. Thank you.

---
