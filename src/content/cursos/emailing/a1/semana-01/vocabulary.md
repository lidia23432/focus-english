## 4) Vocabulary exercises (A1)

### A) Matching (word → category)
Write the category: **job / place / people / communication**
1) manager → ___  
2) office → ___  
3) colleague → ___  
4) email → ___  

**Answers:** 1) job  2) place  3) people  4) communication

### B) Fill in the gaps
1) I work at my ________.  
2) I am in the Sales ________.  
3) Please ________ me if you need help.

**Possible answers:** 1) company  2) team/department  3) contact

### C) Make 6 short sentences
Use these words: **I / am / in / Sales / team / new**
Write 6 short sentences (A1). Example: “I am new.”

Suggested answers (examples)
- I am new.
- I am in Sales.
- I am in the Sales team.
