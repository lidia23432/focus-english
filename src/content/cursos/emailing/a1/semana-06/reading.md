# Emailing A1 — Semana 06 — Reading
**Tema:** Confirmar (Yes/No) + this/that  
**Duración:** 10–15 min

## Texto (A1)
**Subject:** Confirmation

Hi Jordan,  
Yes, that works.  
This time is OK: Tuesday at 14:00.  
Thank you.  
Best regards,  
Alex

## Preguntas
1) What is the subject?  
A) Contact details  B) Confirmation  C) Price list

2) “Yes, that works” means:  
A) Sí, eso funciona / está bien  B) No puedo  C) No entiendo

3) Which time is OK?  
A) Monday at 10:00  B) Tuesday at 14:00  C) Friday at 09:00

4) “This time is OK” refers to:  
A) a file  B) a time  C) a phone number

5) The closing is:  
A) Hi Jordan  B) Thank you  C) Best regards

## Soluciones
1) B  
2) A  
3) B  
4) B  
5) C
