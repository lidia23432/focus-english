## 4) Exercises (A1)

### A) Matching (job → department) (many answers possible)
1) accountant → ___  
2) support agent → ___  
3) HR assistant → ___  
4) technician → ___  

**Possible answers**
1) Finance  2) Support / Customer Service  3) HR  4) IT

### B) Fill in the gaps
1) I am ___ assistant. (a/an)  
2) I am in the ___ department. (Sales/HR/Finance/Support)  
3) Please ___ me if you need help. (contact)

**Possible answers**
1) an  2) Sales/HR/Finance/Support  3) contact

### C) Build 6 short sentences (A1)
Use: I am / a / an / in / department / Sales / HR / assistant / manager
Write 6 short sentences. Example: “I am an assistant.”
