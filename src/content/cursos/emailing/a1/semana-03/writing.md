# Semana 03 — Writing (A1): Roles & departments

## Objetivo (ES)
Escribir emails cortos para presentarte (rol + departamento) y preguntar el rol de otra persona.

---

## 1) Template A (introduce your role)

**Subject:** Introduction – role

Hello __,  
My name is __.  
I am **a/an** __.  
I am in the __ department.  
Nice to meet you.  

Kind regards,  
__

---

## 2) Task A (Guided)
Completa el template con:
- Name: Ana (o tu nombre)
- Role: assistant
- Department: Support
- Recipient: Tom

✅ 6–9 líneas máximo.

---

## 3) Template B (ask about role/department)

**Subject:** Quick question

Hi __,  
What is your role?  
Are you in the __ department?  
Please confirm.  

Best regards,  
__

---

## 4) Task B (Semi-guided)
Escribe un email a **Maria**:
- Pregunta su rol
- Pregunta si está en **Sales**
- Pide confirmación

✅ 5–8 líneas.

---

## 5) Production task (Final)
Write a short email (A1) to a new colleague:
- Say your role (a/an)
- Say your department
- Ask ONE simple question (role or department)
- Add subject + closing

✅ 7–10 líneas.

---

## Extra exercises (for 2h/week)

### A) Reorder (put in correct order)
1) I am in the Sales department.  
2) Subject: Introduction – role  
3) Kind regards,  
4) Hello Maria,  
5) I am an assistant.

**Answer order:** 2 → 4 → 5 → 1 → 3

### B) Make it correct (a/an)
Rewrite:
1) I am a assistant.  
2) I am an manager.

**Possible answers**
1) I am an assistant.  
2) I am a manager.

### C) Shorten (A1 style)
Rewrite into 2–3 short sentences:
- “Hello Maria, my name is Ana and I am an assistant in the Support department and I am new.”

Possible answer:
- Hello Maria. My name is Ana. I am an assistant in the Support department. I am new.

---

## Checklist (A1)
- I use a/an correctly ✅
- I write short sentences ✅
- I include role + department ✅
- I ask politely ✅
- I add closing + my name ✅
