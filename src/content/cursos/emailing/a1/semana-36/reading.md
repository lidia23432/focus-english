# Semana 36 — Reading (A1): Portfolio models (short)

Read the mini models and identify the purpose.

1) “My name is Ana. I am an assistant.” → purpose: introduction  
2) “Please send the price and details.” → purpose: request info  
3) “Please find attached the invoice.” → purpose: attachment  
4) “This is to confirm our meeting on Tuesday at 10:00.” → purpose: confirm meeting  
5) “Sorry for the change. Can we meet on Wednesday at 11:00?” → purpose: reschedule  
6) “This is a reminder: meeting tomorrow at 10:00.” → purpose: reminder  
7) “Any update on the invoice?” → purpose: follow-up  
8) “I can’t open the file. Can you resend it, please?” → purpose: problem/resend

Extra: underline the key phrase in each line.
