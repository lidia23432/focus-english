# Semana 36 — Writing (A1): Final portfolio (8 emails)

## Objetivo (ES)
Crear 8 emails A1 listos para copiar/usar en trabajo.

---

## Portfolio email list (write 8 emails)

### 1) Introduction
Include: name + role + team + closing

### 2) Request information
Include: I need… + Please send… + Please confirm

### 3) Contact details
Include: email/phone/extension + availability (optional)

### 4) Attachment
Include: Please find attached… + confirm receipt

### 5) Confirm meeting
Include: This is to confirm… + on/at + place/call + confirm

### 6) Reschedule
Include: Sorry for the change + not available + new time + confirm

### 7) Reminder
Include: This is a reminder + today/tomorrow + on/at

### 8) Problem + resend + received
Include: I can’t… / doesn’t work + resend request + received + thanks

---

## Final rules (A1)
- subject + greeting + closing
- 1 idea per sentence
- correct on/at
- polite tone
