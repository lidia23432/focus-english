# Semana 36 — Vocabulary (A1): Portfolio keywords

Words: introduction, request, contact details, attachment, invoice, meeting, confirm, reschedule, reminder, follow-up, problem, resend, received, thanks, closing, link

Chunks:
- My name is __. I am a __.
- My phone number is __.
- Please find attached __.
- This is to confirm our meeting on __ at __.
- Sorry for the change.
- This is a reminder.
- Any update on __?
- I can’t open the file.
- Thanks, I received it.
- Kind regards,

Exercise:
Pick 1–2 chunks for each portfolio email before writing.
