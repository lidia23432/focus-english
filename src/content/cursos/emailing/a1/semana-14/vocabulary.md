# Semana 14 — Vocabulary (A1): Meeting confirmation

## 1) Core words (16)
confirm, meeting, call, office, online, link, time, day, date, room, address, available, OK, today, tomorrow, reminder

## 2) Useful chunks (copy/paste)
- This is to confirm our meeting on __ at __.
- We meet at __.
- This is a phone call.
- Here is the link: __
- Is this time OK for you?
- Please confirm.

## 3) Exercises (A1)
A) Matching (confirm/meeting/office/link → ES)
B) Gap-fill (12): confirm/meeting/on/at/OK
C) Build 6 short confirmation sentences (A1).
