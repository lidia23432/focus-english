# Semana 14 — Reading (A1): Confirming a meeting

## Texto 1 (email corto)

**Subject:** Meeting confirmation

Hello Maria,  
This is to confirm our meeting **on Tuesday at 10:00**.  
We meet **at our office**.  
Please confirm.  

Kind regards,  
Tom

---

## Comprehension (A1)

1) Who is the email to?  
a) Tom  b) Maria  c) Tuesday

2) When is the meeting?  
a) Monday at 10:00  
b) Tuesday at 10:00  
c) Tuesday at 12:00

3) Where is the meeting?  
a) At Maria’s home  
b) At our office  
c) Online

4) What does Tom ask Maria to do?  
a) Cancel the meeting  
b) Confirm the meeting  
c) Send a document

**Answers:** 1) b  2) b  3) b  4) b

---

## Texto 2 (email con llamada)

**Subject:** Call on Thursday

Hi Alex,  
This is to confirm our call **on Thursday at 3:00 p.m.**  
Is this time OK for you?  
Thank you,  
Sara

---

## Comprehension (A1)

1) Is it a meeting in an office? Yes / No  
2) What day is the call?  
3) What time is the call?  
4) Write the question Sara uses to ask for confirmation.

**Answers**
1) No  
2) Thursday  
3) 3:00 p.m.  
4) Is this time OK for you?
