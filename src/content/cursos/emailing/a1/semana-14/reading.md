# Semana 14 — Reading (A1): Confirming a meeting

## Reading 1
Subject: Meeting confirmation

Hello Maria,
This is to confirm our meeting on Tuesday at 10:00.
We meet at our office.
Is this time OK for you?
Please confirm.

Kind regards,
Tom

Comprehension (A1)
1) Day: a) Monday b) Tuesday c) Friday
2) Time: a) 10:00 b) 11:00 c) 2:00
3) Place: a) office b) online c) restaurant
4) Tom asks: a) confirm b) pay c) cancel

Answers: 1)b 2)a 3)a 4)a

## Reading 2
Subject: Call confirmation

Hi Tom,
This is to confirm our call on Thursday at 3:30 p.m.
Is this time OK for you?
Please confirm.

Best regards,
Maria

Comprehension (A1)
1) It is a: a) meeting in office b) phone call c) invoice
2) Day: a) Thursday b) Tuesday c) Sunday
3) Time: a) 3:30 p.m. b) 3:00 p.m. c) 4:30 p.m.
4) Maria says: a) Please confirm b) Please resend c) Please stop

Answers: 1)b 2)a 3)a 4)a
