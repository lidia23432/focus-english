# Semana 12 — Vocabulary (A1): Actions

## 1) Core words (16)
send, confirm, check, update, help, call, meet, file, link, document, time, date, today, tomorrow, please, thanks

## 2) Useful chunks
- Can you send __, please?
- Can you confirm __, please?
- Please check __.
- Thank you for your help.

## 3) Exercises
A) Matching (send/confirm/check/update → ES)
B) Gap-fill (12): can / send / confirm / please
C) Build 6 request sentences (A1).
