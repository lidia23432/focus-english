# Semana 12 — Grammar (A1): Can you…?, please?

## Objetivo (ES)
Pedir 2 acciones de forma educada:
- Can you send…?
- Can you confirm…?

---

## 1) Can you…?, please? (A1)
- Can you send the file, please?
- Can you confirm the time, please?

## 2) Please confirm (A1)
- Please confirm.
- Please confirm receipt.

## 3) Mini practice
A) Transform (Please → Can you…?)
1) Please send the document.
→ Can you send the document, please?

2) Please confirm the time.
→ Can you confirm the time, please?

B) Gap-fill
Can you ___ the file, please? (send/ sends)
Answer: send
