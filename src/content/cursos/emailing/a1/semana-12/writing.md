# Emailing A1 — Semana 12 — Writing (Consolidación 3)
**Tema:** Follow-up completo (A1)  
**Duración:** 30–40 min

## Plantilla (A1)
**Subject:** Follow-up

Hi [NAME],  
Just a reminder. This is a follow-up.  
Do you have the [ITEM 1]?  
Do you have the [ITEM 2]?  
Please send it, because I need it [TIME].  
Thank you.  
Best regards,  
[YOUR NAME]

---

## Tarea 1 (principal)
- Destinatario: **Taylor**
- Tu nombre: **Sam**
- Item 1: **price list**
- Item 2: **schedule**
- TIME: **today**

---

## Tarea 2 (variación)
Escribe otro follow-up con:
- Item 1: **the file**
- Item 2: **the schedule**
- TIME: **this week**

---

## Checklist ✅
- Subject correcto
- “Just a reminder / This is a follow-up”
- 2 preguntas “Do you have…?”
- 1 request “Please send it”
- 1 reason con because
- Thank you + cierre
- 7–11 líneas (A1)
